The daily report is a narrative summary of how your store performed on a given day, compared against a baseline of recent comparable days. It highlights the metrics that moved the most, explains likely causes, and breaks key metrics down by dimension so you can decide where to act.

This guide shows you how to open a report, read each section, and regenerate a report when you need a fresh run.

## Prerequisites

- Access to your Consequential account with a connected store.
- At least one day of data so a report can be generated. New stores need enough history before Consequential can build a baseline to compare against.
- To regenerate a report, the **Owner** or **Admin** role on the store.

## View your reports

<Steps>

1. In Consequential, open **Reports** from the sidebar. The page lists your recent reports, most recent first, each with its date and a status.
2. Click a report date to open its detail view.

</Steps>

If the list is empty, no reports have been generated yet. Once your store has enough data, reports appear here for each day.

### Report statuses

Each report in the list shows a status that tells you whether it's ready to read:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Ready** | The report is generated and ready to read. |
| **Generating** | Consequential is building the report. The page refreshes on its own and shows the report when it's ready. |
| **No baseline** | There isn't enough comparable history to build a report for this date yet. |
| **Failed** | Report generation didn't finish. Regenerate the report to try again. |
| **None** | No report exists for this date yet. Open the date to generate one. |

<Aside type="note">
	A report stays in a generating state while Consequential snapshots your data and writes the narrative. The detail page updates automatically — you don't need to refresh your browser.
</Aside>

## Understand each section of a report

A report reads top to bottom, from the headline takeaway down to the supporting detail. Not every section appears in every report — Consequential includes only the sections that are relevant for the day.

### Header

The header shows the report date, when it was generated, and the model that produced it. If you have the **Owner** or **Admin** role, a **Regenerate** button appears here.

### Summary

A short narrative that captures the day's headline story — what changed, by how much, and the most likely cause. Read this first to get the overall picture before you dig into the detail.

### Notable movers

The metrics whose change was statistically unusual compared with their recent history. Consequential surfaces these first because they're the most likely to need your attention.

Each metric card shows:

- The metric's display name and its value for the day.
- The change versus the baseline, as both an absolute amount and a percentage. A green up arrow (▲) marks an increase; a red down arrow (▼) marks a decrease.
- A `z` badge. The z-score measures how far the day's value sits from its baseline — the larger the number, the more unusual the move. Cards with this badge have an outline to stand out.

### Callouts

Plain-language observations that connect the numbers to a likely cause or recommended focus. Each callout has a severity that sets its color:

| Severity | Color | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **High** | Red | Needs attention now — a large or business-critical change. |
| **Medium** | Amber | Worth reviewing — a meaningful but less urgent change. |
| **Low** | Green | Context or a positive signal. |

### Drilldowns

A breakdown of one metric across a dimension — for example, revenue by channel. Each row shows the dimension value, a bar that scales with its size, the value for the day, and the percentage change versus the baseline. Decreases appear in red and increases appear in green. An **Other** row groups the remaining smaller values, and a short narrative below the table calls out what drove the change.

### Context

Supporting signals grouped by source — such as marketing, funnel, or product mix — that help explain the day's results. Each item pairs a short title with a one-line narrative.

### All metrics

A collapsible grid of every metric in the report, not only the notable movers. Click **All metrics** to expand it when you want the full picture, including metrics that stayed flat.

## Regenerate a report

Regenerate a report when generation failed, or when you want a fresh run after you've corrected or updated your underlying data. Regenerating is available to the **Owner** and **Admin** roles.

<Steps>

1. Open the report you want to refresh.
2. Click **Regenerate** in the header. For a date that has no report yet, click **Generate report** instead.
3. Wait for generation to finish. The report moves to a generating state and the page updates on its own when the new version is ready.

</Steps>

A confirmation message tells you that regeneration has started. If a report is already being generated, Consequential lets you know rather than starting a second run.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause and resolution |
| --- | --- |
| The **Regenerate** button is missing | Regenerating requires the **Owner** or **Admin** role. Ask a store owner or admin to run it. |
| The report shows **No baseline** | Your store doesn't yet have enough comparable history for this date. Reports become available as more data accumulates. |
| Generation **Failed** | Click **Regenerate** to try again. If it keeps failing, contact [support@outoftheblue.ai](mailto:support@outoftheblue.ai). |
| "Report is already generating" | A run is already in progress. Wait for it to finish — the page updates on its own. |
