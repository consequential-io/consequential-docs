Your daily report is built from a catalog of **pillars**, **sections**, and
**metrics**. This page is the reference for what that catalog offers: the pillars
your report is organized into, the sections you can turn on inside each one, the
metrics a section can show, and the limits that apply today.

For the step-by-step mechanics — where the editor lives, how switches and pickers
behave, autosave, and plan locks — see
[Configure and schedule daily reports](/daily-reports/configure-and-schedule-daily-reports/).

## The five pillars

Every section belongs to a **pillar** — a themed group shown with a number from
`P1` to `P5`. The pillars are fixed; you choose which sections appear inside
them.

| Pillar | Name | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **P1** | Revenue & profitability | Top-line sales, margin, and profit health. |
| **P2** | Profitable acquisition | New-customer acquisition efficiency: spend, CAC, ROAS, and the paths that pay off. |
| **P3** | Improve conversion | On-site conversion: checkout, cart, product, and quiz funnels. |
| **P4** | Grow active users | Audience reach and engagement: visitors, sessions, page views, and bounce. |
| **P5** | LTV growth | Repeat purchase, retention, and lifetime value. |

## Global details

Above the pillars sits a **Global details** group. These sections read across
every pillar and give you the day's headline story:

- **Headline summary** — a short executive summary of the day across all metrics.
- **Pillar health** — a status line for each pillar, calling out the weakest.
- **Key indicators** — your headline metrics and their change versus the
  comparison baseline.
- **Top movers & draggers** — the biggest positive and negative movers by change.
- **Root cause chain** — the causal chain behind the day's biggest change.
- **Focus areas** — prioritized next actions, each tied to the metric movement
  that triggered it.

Global details sections are **standard**: they appear as written and don't take
metric or breakdown choices.

## Sections you can add to each pillar

Inside a pillar, you turn **sections** on or off and — for editable sections —
choose the metrics they show. The table below lists the editable sections you're
most likely to see for a Shopify store, grouped by pillar.

| Pillar | Editable sections |
| --- | --- |
| **P1** Revenue & profitability | Top Products by sales, Top Products by orders, Channel breakdown |
| **P2** Profitable acquisition | Conversion funnel by GA4, Conversion funnel by Blotout, Acquisition summary |
| **P3** Improve conversion | Buyer breakdown, Lifecycle indicators (Klaviyo), Conversion summary |

<Aside type="note">
	Which sections appear in your editor depends on your plan and on which data
	sources you've connected. A section whose source isn't connected shows a
	**Connect** card instead of its metric picker, and a section locked on your
	plan shows a lock badge. The **Your report** tab in Settings is the source of
	truth for what you can add today.
</Aside>

## Put Top Products in your report

The product sections live in the **P1 Revenue & profitability** pillar. There are
two, so you can show products two ways at once:

- **Top Products by sales** — defaults to total sales.
- **Top Products by orders** — defaults to orders.

To add one:

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings → Reports → Your report**, on the **Composition** step.
2. Expand the **Revenue & profitability** pillar.
3. Turn on **Top Products by sales**, **Top Products by orders**, or both.
4. On a section you turned on, open its **Metrics** picker and select the metrics
   to show.

</Steps>

### Metrics you can choose

A product section always slices by **product**, and its picker offers **sales
metrics**. Among them:

| Metric | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Total sales** | Total sales for each product. |
| **Gross sales** | Sales before discounts and returns. |
| **Net sales** | Sales after discounts and returns. |
| **Orders** | Number of orders containing the product. |
| **Ordered units** | Number of units sold. |
| **Average order value** | Average value of orders containing the product. |

New-customer and repeat-customer variants of these metrics are available too, so
you can rank products by the revenue they bring from first-time buyers.

<Aside type="caution">
	A product section's breakdown is fixed to **product** — you can't switch it to
	show products by a different dimension. You choose the metrics; the product
	breakdown stays.
</Aside>

### Views and inventory aren't available for Top Products

Today the product sections rank products by **sales metrics only**. You can't add
**product views** or **inventory** to a Top Products section:

- **Views** are measured per session, not per product, so they can't be broken
  down by product. Funnel-level view metrics (such as PDP views) appear in the
  **Conversion funnel** sections instead, not against individual products.
- **Inventory** isn't a report metric. Consequential tracks product inventory in
  its data, but it isn't selectable in the report editor.

If a view-by-product or inventory-by-product report would help your team, let us
know at [support@consequential.io](mailto:support@consequential.io) — it helps us
prioritize.

## Report frequency and comparison

Reports run on a **daily** schedule. Each report covers a single day —
yesterday — and compares it against one earlier day. There's no weekly or monthly
cadence: you can turn the daily report on or off and set the time of day it
sends, but not its frequency.

The one lever over how a day reads is the **Comparison** baseline, set on the
**Delivery** tab. A steadier baseline reduces the day-to-day swings that a
low-volume store sees, where a few extra orders can read as a very large
percentage change. The default, **Year over Year (Retail)**, compares against the same
weekday one year ago on the NRF 4-5-4 retail calendar, which controls for both
weekly and seasonal patterns. For the full list of baselines and how to change
them, see
[Set the schedule and audience](/daily-reports/configure-and-schedule-daily-reports/#set-the-schedule-and-audience).

<Aside type="note">
	Each baseline compares one day against one other day. **Week over Week**
	compares yesterday against the same weekday last week — it doesn't add up a
	week of sales into a single total. A weekly **aggregate** (a week's totals
	compared week over week) isn't available today. If your store would read
	better on weekly totals, tell us at
	[support@consequential.io](mailto:support@consequential.io).
</Aside>

## What's not available today

| You might expect | Status |
| --- | --- |
| A weekly or monthly report | Not available — reports are daily only. |
| A weekly aggregate (totals summed over a week) | Not available — every comparison is one day against one day. |
| Changing a product section's breakdown | Not available — product sections always slice by product. |
| Top Products by views | Not available — views aren't measured per product. |
| Top Products by inventory | Not available — inventory isn't a report metric. |
