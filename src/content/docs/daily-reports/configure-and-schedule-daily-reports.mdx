The **Reports** settings page controls how your daily report is built and
delivered: when it sends, which comparison and level of detail it uses, which
metrics sit at the top, who receives it, and whether it arrives by email, Slack,
or both.

This guide shows you how to open the Reports settings, turn the daily report on,
set its schedule, and configure recipients and delivery channels.

## Prerequisites

- The **Owner** or **Admin** role on the workspace. Other roles see the settings
  in read-only mode and can't save changes.
- At least one team member to add as a recipient. Members are managed on the
  **Team** settings page.
- To deliver by Slack, an incoming webhook URL from your Slack workspace.

## Open the reports settings

<Steps>

1. In Consequential, open **Settings**.
2. Select **Reports**.

</Steps>

The page is organized into four sections: **Schedule**, **Pinned metrics**,
**Recipients**, and **Delivery channels**. Changes take effect after you save.

<Aside type="note">
	The **Reports** settings page controls report delivery. To change the wording
	and structure of the report itself, use the **Report prompts** page instead.
</Aside>

## Turn on and schedule the report

The **Daily report enabled** switch is the primary switch. Nothing sends while
it's off, regardless of your other settings. Turn it on to start delivery.

Set the following options under **Schedule**:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Daily report enabled** | Primary switch for delivery. Nothing sends while this is off. Off by default. |
| **Send time** | The time of day the report sends, in 24-hour `HH:MM` format. |
| **Timezone** | The timezone the send time is measured in. Defaults to UTC. |
| **Comparison** | The baseline for comparing each day's metrics. |
| **Report type** | How much detail the report includes. |
| **AI summaries** | Include AI-written summary text in the report. On by default. |

### Comparison options

The comparison sets what "yesterday" is measured against, which shapes every
change shown in the report.

| Comparison | Baseline |
| --- | --- |
| **Same weekday, last year** | The same day of the week one year ago. This is the default and controls for both weekly and seasonal patterns. |
| **Previous period** | The equivalent stretch of days immediately before the report period. |
| **Same date, last year** | The same calendar date one year ago. |

### Report type options

| Report type | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Simple** | A headline summary and pillar health only. |
| **Detailed** | The full root-cause report, with supporting tables and recommended actions. |

## Pin metrics to the top

Under **Pinned metrics**, choose the metrics you want to appear first in every
report, in the order you add them. Pin up to 20 metrics.

<Steps>

1. Select **Add metric**.
2. Choose a metric from the list. It appears as a chip in the order you added it.
3. To remove a pinned metric, select the **✕** on its chip.

</Steps>

## Choose recipients

Under **Recipients**, choose which team members receive the report. Add up to 50
recipients.

<Steps>

1. Select **Add recipient**.
2. Choose a team member from the list. Only active members of your workspace
   appear.
3. To remove a recipient, select the **✕** on their chip.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
	Recipients must be active members of your workspace. Members who are removed
	from the workspace or who have opted out of report emails are dropped
	automatically at send time, so you don't need to prune the list yourself.
</Aside>

## Set delivery channels

Under **Delivery channels**, choose how the report is delivered. You can enable
email, Slack, or both.

| Channel | Setup |
| --- | --- |
| **Email** | Turn on the **Email** toggle. Recipients receive the report at the email address on their account. On by default. |
| **Slack** | Turn on the **Slack** toggle, then enter a **Slack webhook URL**. Off by default. |

### Deliver by Slack

<Steps>

1. Create an incoming webhook in your Slack workspace and copy its URL. It looks
   like `https://hooks.slack.com/...`.
2. On the Reports settings page, turn on the **Slack** toggle.
3. Paste the URL into the **Slack webhook URL** field.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
	When Slack delivery is on, the webhook URL is required and must be a full
	`https://` URL. You can't save the page until you provide a valid URL.
</Aside>

## Save your changes

Select **Save** in the save bar to apply your changes. A confirmation message
tells you the report settings were saved. If a save doesn't go through, check
that any required fields — such as the Slack webhook URL — are filled in, then
try again.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause and resolution |
| --- | --- |
| The form is read-only and **Save** is unavailable | Editing report settings requires the **Owner** or **Admin** role. Ask a workspace owner or admin to make the change. |
| No reports arrive after you turn on delivery | Confirm **Daily report enabled** is on, that you've added at least one recipient, and that you've enabled at least one delivery channel. |
| The page won't save with Slack on | You must enter a valid `https://` **Slack webhook URL** when the Slack channel is enabled. Add the URL, then save. |
| A recipient isn't receiving the report | The member may have been removed from the workspace or opted out of report emails. Confirm they're an active member on the **Team** settings page. |
