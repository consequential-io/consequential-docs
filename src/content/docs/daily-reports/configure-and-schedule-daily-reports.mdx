The **Reports** settings page has two tabs:

- **Your report** — personalize your own report: pick a template, turn sections
  on or off, choose the metrics and breakdowns each section shows, preview it
  live, and email yourself a sample. Every member can edit their own report.
- **Delivery** — set the schedule, recipients, and channels for the whole
  workspace. Only **Owner** and **Admin** roles can change these; other roles
  see them in read-only mode.

This guide covers both, starting with building your report.

## Open the report editor

<Steps>

1. In Consequential, open **Settings**.
2. Select **Reports**, then the **Your report** tab.

</Steps>

The editor has three steps, shown as linked boxes: **Composition** (choose what
appears), **Prompts** (tune the AI wording), and **Test** (email yourself a
sample).

## Turn your report on

Two switches sit at the top of **Composition**:

| Switch | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Receive this report** | Your personal on/off switch for the daily report. |
| **AI summaries** | Include the AI-written narrative. Tables always show either way. On by default. |

<Aside type="note">
	You receive a report only when **both** your **Receive this report** switch
	and the workspace's **Daily report enabled** switch (on the **Delivery** tab)
	are on, and you are listed as a recipient.
</Aside>

## Choose a template

Your report starts from a **template** tied to a persona — for example
**Founder** or **Marketer**. Each template is a ready-made layout of sections
tuned to that role: the Founder template covers the whole business, while the
Marketer template focuses on acquisition and conversion. A short description
sits under each option.

Use the **Report template** dropdown to pick the one closest to how you work,
then customize it from there.

<Aside type="caution">
	Switching template **replaces** your current report with the new template's
	layout and **discards your customizations**. You confirm with **Switch &
	reset** before it applies. This change saves immediately — the Discard action
	cannot undo it.
</Aside>

## Understand pillars and sections

Your report is organized into **pillars** — themed groups such as *Revenue &
profitability* or *Profitable acquisition*, each shown with a number like `P1`.
Inside a pillar sit its **sections**: the individual tables and summaries.

- **Global details** — a group at the top for the summary and recommended
  actions that apply across every pillar.
- Each pillar shows how many of its sections are on (for example, `3/5 on`) and
  a switch that turns all of its sections on or off at once. Expand a pillar to
  see and adjust its sections one by one.

This is the pillar-to-section mapping: every section belongs to one pillar, or
to **Global details** when it spans all of them.

## What each section shows

Turn a section on or off with its checkbox. Each section is one of two kinds,
marked by a chip:

| Kind | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Table** | A set of metrics laid out over one dimension — for example, top products by sales. It may carry extra chips such as *sliced by product title* or *for source klaviyo*. |
| **Summary** | A short written narrative, plus optional metric breakdowns. |

A small icon next to the name tells you whether you can customize it:

- **Editable** (sliders icon) — you choose the metrics and breakdowns shown here.
- **Standard** (badge icon) — this section is fixed and cannot be changed.

A one-line description under each name explains what the section covers.

## Choose metrics and breakdowns

For an **editable** section, the pickers appear once the section is on:

- **Table** → **Metrics**. Open it and select the metrics for the table.
- **Summary** → **Metrics** (the figures the narrative can cite) and **Add
  breakdown**. For a breakdown, pick a dimension, then choose the metrics to
  show for it. Add more than one — they appear as **Breakdowns · N**.

<Aside type="note">
	If a section needs a data source you have not connected yet, you see a
	**Connect** card in place of the metric picker. Select **Connect** to set the
	source up on the **Connectors** page, then return to the report editor.
</Aside>

Some sections use every metric or every dimension by design. These have no
picker — they always show the full set.

## Preview your report live

As you edit, the **Live preview** panel on the right redraws your report
automatically — there is no button to press. It uses **sample numbers**, not
your real data, so you can see the layout and wording of each change right away.
Turn a section on, pick a metric, or switch template, and the preview updates
within a moment.

## Autosave

Your changes save on their own about a second after you stop editing, and an
**Autosaved** confirmation appears. A **Save** / **Discard** bar is also
available if you want to save or roll back immediately.

<Aside type="note">
	Switching **template** is the one change that saves immediately and cannot be
	rolled back with **Discard** — confirm it with care.
</Aside>

## Tune the AI wording (Prompts)

The **Prompts** step lets you tune *how* the AI writes each section — for
example, ask it to *lead with margin*, note the *gap to plan*, or *call out the
biggest driver*. You pick a section on the left and add your guidance on the
right.

The AI never computes a number. This only changes how the summary reads — every
figure still comes from your live data and the metrics you chose in
**Composition**.

<Aside type="caution">
	The **Prompts** step is available on higher plans. On your current plan it
	shows a **Locked** badge and *Available on a higher plan*, and the ✨ button on
	each section is inactive. Upgrade to tune per-section wording.
</Aside>

## Email yourself a sample

Open the **Test** step. It has two separate actions:

- **Email me a sample → Send now.** Sends a one-off preview of your **current**
  settings to your own inbox, right now. It uses **mock numbers** and goes only
  to you. If you send several in a row, you are asked to wait a few minutes
  before sending again.
- **Schedule daily delivery → Schedule daily email.** Opens the **Delivery**
  tab, where you set up the **real** recurring report with your live data.

<Aside type="note">
	The sample is a preview for checking layout and wording — not the real daily
	report, and not sent to anyone but you.
</Aside>

## Sections locked on your plan

Some sections may be locked on your current plan. A locked section shows a lock
badge with the plan it needs, and a banner tells you how many are locked. Locked
sections are never included in your report. Upgrade to add them.

## Schedule and deliver

The **Delivery** tab sets the schedule, recipients, and channels for the whole
workspace.

### Prerequisites

- The **Owner** or **Admin** role. Other roles see the Delivery tab in read-only
  mode and cannot save changes.
- At least one team member to add as a recipient. Members are managed on the
  **Team** settings page.
- To deliver by Slack, an incoming webhook URL from your Slack workspace.

### Set the schedule and audience

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Daily report enabled** | The workspace switch. Nothing sends while it is off, whatever else is set. |
| **Send time** | The time of day the report sends, in 24-hour `HH:MM` format. |
| **Comparison** | The baseline each day is measured against (see below). |
| **Recipients** | The workspace members who receive the report. |
| **Delivery channels** | Email, Slack, or both. |

The **Comparison** options are:

| Comparison | Baseline |
| --- | --- |
| **Same weekday, last year** | The same day of the week one year ago. The default — it controls for both weekly and seasonal patterns. |
| **Previous period** | The equivalent stretch of days immediately before the report period. |
| **Same date, last year** | The same calendar date one year ago. |
| **Previous day** | The single day before. Use this to track short-term momentum. |

<Steps>

1. Turn on **Daily report enabled**.
2. Set the **Send time** and **Comparison**.
3. Under **Recipients**, add the members who should receive the report. Only
   active members appear. Removed or opted-out members are dropped automatically
   at send time.
4. Under **Delivery channels**, turn on **Email**, **Slack**, or both. For
   Slack, paste an incoming webhook URL that starts with `https://`.
5. Select **Save**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
	When Slack is on, a valid `https://` webhook URL is required — you cannot save
	the page without it.
</Aside>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause and resolution |
| --- | --- |
| The **Delivery** tab is read-only | Only **Owner** and **Admin** roles can change delivery settings. The **Your report** tab stays editable for every member. |
| The **Prompts** step is locked | Per-section wording is available on higher plans. Upgrade to unlock it. |
| A section shows **Connect** instead of pickers | That section's data source is not connected. Select **Connect**, set it up on the **Connectors** page, then return. |
| No sample arrives from **Send now** | Confirm your account has an email address. If you sent several in a row, wait a few minutes and try again. |
| No daily report arrives | Confirm your **Receive this report** switch and the workspace **Daily report enabled** switch are both on, that you are a recipient, and that at least one delivery channel is enabled. |
