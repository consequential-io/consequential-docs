After your store is connected, connect the rest of your data: your ad accounts, Google Analytics 4 and your email tool. You can do it from the **Onboarding** card on Home or from **Settings → Connectors**. Both show the same list of sources. Only Owners and Admins can connect data.

## Prerequisites

- A connected store. See [Connect your store](/get-started/connect-your-store/).
- The Owner or Admin role in your workspace.
- Access to the ad, analytics and email accounts you want to connect.

## Connect from the Onboarding card

<Steps>

1. On Home, click the **Onboarding** card.
2. Click the step you want: **Connect your ad accounts**, **Connect Google Analytics 4** or **Connect your email tool**. A dialog opens with the sources for that step.
3. Click **Connect** next to the platform you use, and follow the prompts to give Consequential access.
4. Click **Continue**.

</Steps>

| Step | Required | Can you skip it? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Connect your ad accounts | Yes | No |
| Connect Google Analytics 4 | No | Yes. Click **Skip for now**. |
| Connect your email tool | No | Yes. Click **Skip for now**. |

A skipped step shows as striped on the card's bar. To bring it back, open the card and click **Undo** next to the step.

<Aside type="note">
Until your store is connected, the sources are turned off and the dialog says *"Connect your platform first to enable these."*
</Aside>

## Connect from Settings → Connectors

<Steps>

1. Click **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Connectors**.
2. Under **Data sources**, find your platform. Use the search box, or page through the list.
3. Click **Connect**, and follow the prompts to give Consequential access.

</Steps>

A connected source shows **Connected** and the date it was connected. **+ Add connection** adds another connection for the same platform, and **Disconnect** removes it. Sources marked coming soon are set up for you by our team.

The **Platform** section at the top shows your store. If it says **Disconnected**, click **Reconnect**.

## After you connect

Each connection marks its step on the Onboarding card as done. Your data takes a while to load the first time. Until it does, Acquisition shows *"No acquisition data yet. Your first refresh is still running."*

If a source stops updating later, Home shows a data alert, for example *"Your analytics data is not up to date."*, and the step on the Onboarding card shows **Not up to date**.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The sources are turned off and say *"Connect your platform first to enable these."* | Connect your store first. See [Connect your store](/get-started/connect-your-store/). |
| You can't connect anything | Only Owners and Admins can connect data. Ask an Owner or Admin of your workspace. |
| A data alert says a source is not up to date | Open **Settings → Connectors** and check the source. If your store shows **Disconnected**, click **Reconnect**. |
