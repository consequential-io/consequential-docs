You connect Meta Ads yourself. Only Owners and Admins can connect data, and your store must be connected first.

<Steps>

1. On Home, open the **Onboarding** card and click **Connect your ad accounts**. Or go to **Settings → Connectors** and find **Facebook Ads** under **Data sources**.
2. Click **Connect** next to **Facebook Ads**, and follow the prompts to give Consequential access.
3. When it shows **Connected**, you're done. The step on the Onboarding card is marked as done.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
If you have any questions or run into issues, contact [support@consequential.io](mailto:support@consequential.io).
</Aside>
