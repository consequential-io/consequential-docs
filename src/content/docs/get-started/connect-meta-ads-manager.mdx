This guide helps you connect Out of the Blue to your Meta Ads Manager account so our platform can access your campaign performance data. This connection enables Out of the Blue to monitor ad signals, detect issues, and provide accurate insights and reports.

## Meta access setup

<Steps>

1. Go to [Meta Business Settings](https://business.facebook.com/settings).

2. Under **Users > People**, click the **+** icon to invite people.

3. Enter `connect@outoftheblue.ai` and click **Next**.

4. Under **Ad Accounts**, select the relevant ad account and check **View performance** (you only need this permission).

5. Click **Invite**. We receive the invite and accept it on our end.

</Steps>
