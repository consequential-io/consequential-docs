This section covers connecting Consequential to your store and marketing platforms. You connect most platforms yourself. Sources marked coming soon are set up by our team.

## What's inside

- [Onboarding](/get-started/onboarding/) — the Getting started cards on Home, the sidebar, and Settings.
- [Create your account and connect your store](/get-started/create-your-account/)
- [Connect your store](/get-started/connect-your-store/) — Shopify or another store platform.
- [Connect your data](/get-started/connect-your-data/) — your ad accounts, Google Analytics 4 and email tool.
- [Connect Shopify](/get-started/install-shopify-app/)
- [Connect Meta Ads](/get-started/connect-meta-ads-manager/)
- [Connect Google Ads](/get-started/connect-google-ads/)
- [Connect Google Analytics 4](/get-started/connect-ga4/)
- [Add Consequential to your Shopify store (optional)](/get-started/add-shopify-staff/)

## Who this is for

This guide is for marketing managers, eCommerce operations teams, and data analysts responsible for tracking performance, managing integrations, and maintaining data accuracy across platforms.

<Aside type="tip">
Once your data is connected, you can query it from Claude or ChatGPT through Consequential's MCP server. See [Connect your AI assistant to Consequential Analytics (MCP)](/academy/mcp-guide/connect-your-ai-assistant-to-consequential-analytics/).
</Aside>
