Your store is the first thing you connect. The first time you sign in, Consequential opens the **Connect your store** dialog. You can also open it later from the **Onboarding** card on Home. Only Owners and Admins can connect a store.

## Prerequisites

- A Consequential account. See [Create your account and connect your store](/get-started/create-your-account/).
- For Shopify: permission to install apps on your Shopify store.
- For other platforms: your store's web address (for example, `acme.com`).

## Connect a Shopify store

<Steps>

1. In the **Connect your store** dialog, click **Install on Shopify**. Shopify opens in a new tab.
2. Approve the Consequential app in Shopify. If Shopify shows **Finish setup in Consequential**, click it.
3. Go back to Consequential. The dialog updates by itself and shows *"Your Shopify store is connected to Consequential."*
4. Click **Continue**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
When you install the Consequential app from Shopify, Consequential reads your store's country from Shopify, so it doesn't ask you for it.
</Aside>

## Connect a store on another platform

<Steps>

1. In the **Connect your store** dialog, click **I'm not on Shopify**.
2. Under **What do you run your store on?**, choose your platform, for example WooCommerce.
3. Under **Where is this store based?**, choose the country the store mainly sells from.
4. Under **Your store address**, enter your store's web address (for example, `acme.com`).
5. Click **Continue**.

</Steps>

We set up your workspace and get in touch about connecting your orders. You can connect your ad platforms straight away.

Your country sets the store's name (for example, `Acme US`), its reporting currency and its timezone. You can change the currency later. See [Set a store's currency](/how-tos/onboarding/manage-store-preferences/#set-a-stores-currency).

## Request access to a connected store

If the store is already connected to another workspace, Consequential shows **Store already connected**: *"This store is already connected to another workspace. You can request access from an owner, or try a different URL."*

You have two options:

- To join the existing workspace, click **Request access**. Consequential shows *"Request sent — an owner will review it."* An owner of that workspace reviews and approves your request.
- To connect a different store instead, click **Try a different URL**.

<Aside type="note">
If you're already a member of the workspace that owns the store, Consequential skips the request and takes you straight to that workspace.
</Aside>

## What happens next

After your store is connected, you land on Home. The **Onboarding** card shows **Connect your store** as done, and its next step is **Connect your ad accounts**. See [Connect your data](/get-started/connect-your-data/), and [Onboarding](/get-started/onboarding/) for every step on the Getting started cards.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause and resolution |
| --- | --- |
| "We couldn't connect that store. Please check the address and try again." | Check the store address, then click **Continue** again. |
| "Too many attempts — please try again in a moment." | You tried several times in quick succession. Wait a moment, then try again. |
| "Store already connected" | The store is connected to another workspace. Click **Request access** to join it, or **Try a different URL** to connect a different store. |
| "We couldn't send that request. Please try again." | Consequential couldn't send your access request. Click **Request access** again. |
