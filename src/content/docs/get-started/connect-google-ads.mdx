This guide will help you connect Out of the Blue to your Google Ads account so our team can analyze your ad performance and provide accurate insights. Follow the steps below to securely grant access by adding our user account and allowing the Out of the Blue domain.

## How to connect Out of the Blue with your Google Ads

<Steps>

1. Go to **Admin → Access & Security → Add new User**.

2. Email id: `connect@outoftheblue.ai`

3. Access level: **Standard**

4. Go to **Admin → Access & Security → Allow Domains**.

5. Include the `outoftheblue.ai` domain in the list.

</Steps>
