Google Ads isn't a self-serve connector — Consequential's team connects it for you as part of onboarding. During the **Connect your data** step, Google Ads shows a **Managed setup** status rather than a self-serve connect button.

<Aside type="note">
If Google Ads tracking is detected on your storefront during the onboarding scan, it's automatically added to your list and prioritized for setup. You don't need to do anything else to request it.
</Aside>

If you'd like Google Ads connected sooner, or want to check on its status, contact [support@consequential.io](mailto:support@consequential.io).
