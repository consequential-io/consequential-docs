This guide explains how to add a staff member to your Shopify store. Adding a staff account for Consequential is optional but recommended, as it allows our team to cross-validate your store data and provide more accurate insights.

## Quick steps: add a Consequential account on Shopify

<Steps>

1. Log in to your Shopify Admin: `https://your-store.myshopify.com/admin`

2. Go to **Settings** from the main navigation.

3. Click on **Users and permissions**.

4. Click the blue **Add staff** button.

5. Enter the staff member's email and select the permissions you want to assign.

6. Click **Send invite**. They receive an email to join.

7. Once accepted, their account appears under **Users and permissions**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
This is an optional step. Adding Consequential as staff on your Shopify store helps us cross-validate your numbers.

If you have any questions, contact [support@consequential.io](mailto:support@consequential.io).
</Aside>
