This guide walks you through your first run in Consequential: sign in, connect your store, and land on Home. Consequential starts your free trial and sets up your workspace in the background. The rest of setup is tracked by the **Getting started** cards on Home. See [Onboarding](/get-started/onboarding/).

## Prerequisites

- A work email address, or a Google account to sign in with.
- For Shopify: permission to install apps on your store. For other platforms: your store's web address (for example, `acme.com`).

## Sign in

There is no separate sign-up form. Your account is created the first time you sign in.

<Steps>

1. Go to [app.consequential.io](https://app.consequential.io).
2. Click **Continue with Google**, or **Log in with SSO** if your company uses single sign-on. Or type your email and click **Email me a sign-in link**.
3. If you asked for a link, open the email and click the link. It signs you in.

</Steps>

## Connect your store

The first time you sign in, Consequential opens the **Connect your store** dialog.

- On Shopify, click **Install on Shopify** and approve the app in Shopify.
- On another platform, click **I'm not on Shopify**, then choose your platform and country and enter your store address.

See [Connect your store](/get-started/connect-your-store/) for each step.

What happens next depends on whether your store is already connected to a Consequential workspace:

| Situation | What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your store is new to Consequential | Consequential sets up your workspace | Continue to [Set up your workspace](#set-up-your-workspace). |
| Your store belongs to a workspace you're a member of | Consequential opens that workspace | Nothing. You go straight to it. |
| Your store belongs to a workspace you're *not* a member of | A **Store already connected** message | [Request access](#request-access-to-an-existing-workspace) or try a different URL. |

## Set up your workspace

When your store is new, Consequential binds it to your workspace, starts your **free trial** and starts loading your data. You land on Home.

Home is usable right away. Until your first data load finishes, some pages show that it is still running.

On Home, the **Getting started** cards track the rest of setup:

| Card | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Onboarding** | Connect your store, ad accounts, Google Analytics 4 and email tool, check your data, and our team's review. See [Connect your data](/get-started/connect-your-data/). |
| **Setup Profile** | Connect an AI assistant, set up your daily report and notifications, invite your team, and pin your key metrics. |
| **Setup Acquisition tab** | Set your profit margin and grading rules. Shows only when the Acquisition tab is on for your workspace. |
| **Explore additional features** | Features that are coming soon. |

Each card remembers your progress, and you can come back to it at any time. See [Onboarding](/get-started/onboarding/) for every step.

## Request access to an existing workspace

If your store is already connected to a workspace you don't belong to, Consequential shows a **Store already connected** message instead of setting up a new workspace. This prevents duplicate workspaces for the same store.

<Steps>

1. Click **Request access**. Consequential notifies a workspace owner and shows **Request sent — an owner will review it**.
2. Wait for an owner to approve your request. When they do, you become a member and can open the workspace.

</Steps>

If you entered the wrong store, click **Try a different URL**.

<Aside type="note">
Workspace owners approve access requests from team management. See [Manage your team](/account-management/manage-your-team/).
</Aside>

## Verify your setup

You've finished the first run when:

- You can reach Home.
- The **Onboarding** card shows **Connect your store** as done.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause and resolution |
| --- | --- |
| "We couldn't connect that store. Please check the address and try again." | Check the store address, then click **Continue** again. |
| "Too many attempts — please try again in a moment." | You tried several times in quick succession. Wait a moment, then try again. |
| **Store already connected** | Your store belongs to a workspace you're not a member of. [Request access](#request-access-to-an-existing-workspace) or try a different URL. |
