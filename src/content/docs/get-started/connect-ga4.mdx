This guide walks you through the steps to connect Out of the Blue to your Google Analytics 4 (GA4) account. Granting access allows our team to analyze your data accurately and provide deeper insights into your website and marketing performance.

## Quick steps: add Out of the Blue to your GA4 account

<Steps>

1. Go to **Admin → Account Access Management → Add new User**.

2. Email id: `connect@outoftheblue.ai`

3. Access level: **Analyst**

</Steps>
