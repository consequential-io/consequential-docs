Google Analytics 4 isn't a self-serve connector — Consequential's team connects it for you as part of onboarding. During the **Connect your data** step, GA4 shows a **Managed setup** status rather than a self-serve connect button.

<Aside type="note">
If GA4 tracking is detected on your storefront during the onboarding scan, it's automatically added to your list and prioritized for setup. You don't need to do anything else to request it.
</Aside>

If you'd like GA4 connected sooner, or want to check on its status, contact [support@consequential.io](mailto:support@consequential.io).
