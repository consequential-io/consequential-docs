This guide walks you through the steps to install the Out of the Blue app from the Shopify App Store. By connecting your Shopify store, you enable seamless data integration and gain access to advanced analytics, insights, and monitoring features. Follow the step-by-step instructions in the following sections to complete the installation and choose the plan that best fits your business needs.

## Quick steps: Install the Out of the Blue app on Shopify

<Steps>

1. Go to the Out of the Blue app listing on the Shopify App Store.

2. Click the **Install** button to begin the installation process for your selected Shopify store.

3. Review the app permissions and details about the access Out of the Blue requests.

4. Optionally, click to learn more about app permissions and review the privacy policy if you wish.

5. Click **Install** again to finalize and proceed with app setup.

6. After installation, Out of the Blue redirects you to its channel for further configuration.

7. Select a subscription plan that suits your business needs (we recommend the Growth plan to start).

</Steps>
