Shopify is the one connector you set up yourself, from the **Connect your data** step of onboarding.

<Steps>

1. On the Shopify card, click **Connect**.
2. Confirm your Shopify store domain, in the form `your-store.myshopify.com`.
3. Click **Continue to Shopify**.
4. On Shopify, review the access Consequential requests and approve it.
5. Wait for Shopify to send you back to onboarding. The Shopify card now shows a **Connected** badge.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
If you're connecting an additional store to an existing Consequential account rather than doing first-time onboarding, contact [support@consequential.io](mailto:support@consequential.io) to get it added.
</Aside>
