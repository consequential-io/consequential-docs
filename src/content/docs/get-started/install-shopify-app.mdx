title: Connect Shopify
description: Connect your Shopify store to Consequential to power your dashboards.
---

import { Steps, Aside } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

You connect Shopify yourself, from the **Connect your store** dialog. It opens the first time you sign in, and you can open it later from the **Onboarding** card on Home.

<Steps>

1. In the **Connect your store** dialog, click **Install on Shopify**. Shopify opens in a new tab.
2. On Shopify, review the access Consequential requests and approve it. If Shopify shows **Finish setup in Consequential**, click it.
3. Go back to Consequential. The dialog updates by itself and shows *"Your Shopify store is connected to Consequential."*
4. Click **Continue**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
To connect another store later, use **Add Store** in the store switcher at the top left. If you have any questions, contact [support@consequential.io](mailto:support@consequential.io).
</Aside>
