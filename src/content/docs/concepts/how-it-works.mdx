## Core objects

Consequential is organized around a small set of objects:

- **Tenant** — the top-level container, one per brand (for example, "ThirdLove").
  Every user, store, integration, and metric belongs to exactly one tenant.
- **Store** — a specific storefront under a tenant (for example a brand's US, UK,
  DE, and CA storefronts). Most dashboards and metrics are viewed for one store
  at a time, using the store switcher at the top of the app.
- **User** — a person who signs in with Google OAuth and is attached to one or
  more tenants with a role such as Admin.
- **Metric** — a defined KPI (CAC, ROAS, MER, AOV, and similar) computed from
  your connected data. See the [Metrics](/academy/metrics/) section for
  definitions of each one.
- **Attribution model** — the rule used to decide which channel gets credit for
  a conversion (last-click, first-click, or platform-reported). See
  [Attribution](/academy/attribution/attribution-models/).
- **Daily report** — an automated, AI-generated summary of a store's previous
  day emailed to a tenant's team. See the
  [Daily reports](/guides/daily-reports/) guide.

## How data flows

```
Connect a source (Shopify, Meta, Google Ads, GA4, ...)
        ↓
Consequential ingests and aggregates the data
        ↓
Surfaces on Home, Metrics, and the Acquisition Dashboard
        ↓
Optionally summarized each morning in your Daily Report
```

Shopify is connected by installing the Consequential app from your store's
admin. Ad and analytics platforms (Meta, Google Ads, GA4) are currently
connected by Consequential's team on your behalf — see
[Get started](/get-started/overview/).

## Your Home dashboard

Signing in lands you on **Home**, which is what a new workspace looks like
before you've connected everything:

- **Sidebar** — under Dashboard: **Home**, **Analyze**, and **Metrics**. Below
  that: Notifications, Upgrade, Support, and Settings.
- **Workspace switcher** (top left) — pick which tenant and store you're
  viewing, for example "ThirdLove US."
- **Date range picker** (top right) — the window every metric and chart on the
  page is computed over.
- **Finish setting up your workspace** — a short checklist that tracks your
  onboarding progress: connect a source, tell us about your business, invite
  your team, connect ad & analytics sources, and pin your first metric. A
  "Watch the walkthrough" video sits above the checklist but isn't counted in
  its progress.
- **Pinned metrics** — empty until you star a metric from the
  [Metrics](/academy/metrics/) page; starred metrics then show up here as an
  at-a-glance summary every time you open Home.

<Aside type="tip">
	If Home looks mostly empty, that usually means no metrics are pinned yet
	rather than something being broken — head to Metrics and star a few.
</Aside>
