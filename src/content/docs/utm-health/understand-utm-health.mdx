UTM Health scores how clean your campaign tracking is and shows you exactly where it breaks. Consequential checks the UTM parameters on your orders and sessions, grades them against a set of health checks, and lists the specific issues that cost you attribution — missing tags, inconsistent casing, self-referrals, and more.

UTM Health is available to every Consequential plan. It was previously a paid add-on; you no longer need an entitlement to use it.

## Prerequisites

- A connected Shopify store and traffic with UTM parameters to score. See [Connect your data](/get-started/connect-your-data/).
- New to UTM parameters? Read [Understanding UTM tracking](/faq/advertising-best-practices/understanding-utm-tracking/) first.

## Where to find it

In Consequential, open **UTM Health** from the sidebar.

<Aside type="note">
If you don't see **UTM Health** in the sidebar, the feature is switched off for your workspace. It's available on every plan, so this is rare — contact [Consequential support](mailto:support@consequential.io) if you expect it and it's missing.
</Aside>

## Choose what to score

Use the **Orders / Sessions** toggle to switch what Consequential scores:

- **Orders** — the UTM parameters attached to your orders.
- **Sessions** — the UTM parameters attached to site sessions.

The **Showing** label under the title tells you which date range the report covers. UTM Health uses your global date range when you set one, and the last 30 days otherwise.

## Read the score

The page opens with your overall **UTM Health Score** (0–100), then breaks it down:

<Steps>

1. **The score** — a single 0–100 number summarizing how clean your tracking is for the selected entity and date range.
2. **Category scores** — the overall score is grouped into categories, each with its own score, so you can see which area needs the most work.
3. **Checks** — each category is made up of individual checks. Every check shows a status of **Healthy**, **Warning**, or **Critical**, a percentage, and the detail behind it.

</Steps>

Consequential runs these checks:

| Check | What it looks for |
| --- | --- |
| UTM coverage | The share of orders or sessions that carry UTM parameters at all. |
| Parameter completeness | Whether the expected UTM parameters (source, medium, campaign, and others) are present. |
| Source/medium validity | Whether `utm_source` and `utm_medium` hold valid, recognized values. |
| Casing uniformity | Whether the same value appears in mixed casing (for example, `Facebook` and `facebook`). |
| Naming consistency | Whether campaign and channel names follow a consistent naming pattern. |
| Self-referral detection | Traffic that your own domain is tagged as referring, which distorts attribution. |
| Landing page available | Whether a landing page is recorded for the traffic. |

## The report card

After the score, the report card lists the problems Consequential found, check by check, with the specific values behind each one. From the report card you can:

- **Export a CSV** of a section's detail rows to work through the issues outside Consequential.
- **Open the score settings** with the gear control, which takes you to **Settings → UTM Score**.

<Aside type="caution">
Automatic UTM fixes aren't available in Consequential yet. UTM Health shows you what to fix; you make the changes in your ad platform and store.
</Aside>

## Adjust the score weights

The overall score is a weighted blend of the checks. To change how much each area counts, open **Settings → Analytics → UTM Score** (or use the gear on the report card).

On that page, adjust the weights for each check group and save. The next report recalculates the score with your weights.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause and resolution |
| --- | --- |
| **UTM Health** isn't in the sidebar | The feature is switched off for your workspace. It's available on every plan — contact [Consequential support](mailto:support@consequential.io). |
| The page says there's nothing in this range | No orders or sessions carry UTM data for the selected entity and range. Pick a wider date range. |
| A banner says only the largest groups are scored | The range has more UTM combinations than one report can hold. Choose a shorter range for a complete report. |
| The score changed after I edited the settings | Expected. The score is recalculated from the weights you set under **Settings → UTM Score**. |
