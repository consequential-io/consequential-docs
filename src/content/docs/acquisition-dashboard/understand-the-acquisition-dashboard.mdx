The Acquisition Dashboard answers one question: which channels, campaigns, ad sets, creatives, and landing pages are driving revenue and new customers — and at what spend efficiency. It covers paid and organic acquisition together, not just ad platforms.

## Prerequisites

- A connected Shopify store and at least one connected ad datasource.
- Consequential's daily data pipeline needs to have run at least once. Until then, some rows may show as pending or stale.

## Where to find it

In Consequential, open **Analyze → Acquisition**. The dashboard opens on the **Channels** tab by default.

## The five tabs

<Steps>

1. **Channels** — every dollar of revenue classified into a taxonomy: Paid Ads, Email Marketing, Organic, Direct, Referrals, Marketplaces, Unattributed, and Others. Rows expand from category to sub-category to daily detail.
2. **Campaigns**, **Ad sets**, and **Creatives** — entity-level performance for your connected ad platforms, with letter grades, performance segments, and recommended actions (see below). **Creatives** rows link to a detail page with a daily performance trend for that creative.
3. **Pages** — landing-page performance, one row per ad-and-page pair.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
Platform-reported metrics (spend, clicks, impressions, CTR, and the platform's own revenue/ROAS numbers) are only available on Campaigns, Ad sets, and Creatives — the Channels and Pages tabs don't have a platform-data equivalent.
</Aside>

## Filters

- **Period** — 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, month-to-date, or year-to-date.
- **Attribution model** — Last click, First click, or the platform's own reported ("data-driven") numbers.
- **Compare to previous period** — toggles a comparison column against the prior equivalent period.
- **Ad provider** and **campaign status** filters — campaign status only applies to the Campaigns, Ad sets, and Creatives tabs.

## Reading the metrics

- **Spend, clicks, impressions, CTR** — platform-reported, unaffected by attribution model.
- **Revenue, orders, ROAS, CPA, CVR** — each comes in two forms: what the ad platform itself reports, and what Consequential attributes based on your selected attribution model. These can differ, especially under Last click vs. First click.
- **New vs. returning customer split** — revenue, CAC, ROAS, and AOV are all available broken out by new customers (`NC`) and returning customers (`RC`).
- **CAC** — ad spend divided by orders. See the [CAC](/academy/metrics/cac/), [NC-CAC](/academy/metrics/nc-cac/), and [RC-CAC](/academy/metrics/rc-cac/) metric pages for exact formulas.
- **POAS and MER** — profitability-based metrics, only shown once your tenant has profitability configured (see Settings below). See [POAS](/academy/metrics/poas/) and [MER](/academy/metrics/mer/).
- **iROAS** — incremental ROAS, comparing this period's ROAS to the prior period's.
- **Match rate** — the share of orders each ad provider was able to match back to its own click or view data.

## Grades and recommended actions

On the Campaigns, Ad sets, and Creatives tabs, each row gets a letter grade (A–D) built from CTR, ROAS, CVR, and other signals, plus:

- A **performance segment** — Winners, High potential, or Iteration needed.
- A **recommended action** based on that segment.
- A **percentile rank** against comparable entities.
- A daily grade history you can drill into (Creatives link to a full detail page for this).

## Settings

Under **Settings → Acquisition**:

- **Order-side attribution** — when this is off, the whole dashboard falls back to platform-only numbers (spend, clicks, impressions, CTR, CPM); attributed revenue, CAC, and the new/returning split all require this to be on.
- **Profitability inputs** — set your ad spend percentage and contribution margin percentage here to unlock the POAS and MER columns. Leaving either blank hides those columns entirely, rather than showing an estimate.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause and resolution |
| --- | --- |
| The dashboard is empty | You need a connected Shopify store and at least one connected ad datasource. See [Connect your data](/get-started/connect-your-data/) to check what's connected. |
| Data looks out of date | Data refreshes once a day, so the most recent day or two may still be processing. If the data is more than a few days behind, a source may have stopped updating even though it still shows **Connected** — see [Spot a source that stopped updating](/account-management/manage-your-connected-data-sources/#spot-a-source-that-stopped-updating). |
| POAS and MER columns are missing | Set both your ad spend percentage and contribution margin percentage under **Settings → Acquisition**. |
| Attributed numbers look very different from platform numbers | Expected — they're computed under your selected attribution model, not the platform's own methodology. Try switching between Last click, First click, and platform-reported to compare. |
