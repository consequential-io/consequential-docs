Notable changes to Consequential, newest first. Each change is listed under the
month it reached the app at [app.consequential.io](https://app.consequential.io).

<Aside>
Some features are switched on workspace by workspace. If an item is marked
**Rolling out** or **Early access**, your workspace may not show it yet. Ask your
Consequential contact if you want it turned on.
</Aside>

## November 2026

### Added

- **Ask about your metrics** (Rolling out): ask a question about a metric from
  the Insights tab, or select **Research it** on an insight card. The answer
  comes with a chart of the period you asked about and its forecast band.
- **Notes on charts**: add a note against a date range under a metric chart or
  an insight chart, for example to mark a promotion or a site outage, so your
  team sees what happened on those days.
- **Display currency**: switch the currency figures are shown in on Acquisition
  and Metrics. Nine currencies are available.
- **Custom MER and Custom NC MER**: two optional blended metrics that don't
  depend on attribution. Turn them on from **Settings › Attribution**, a new
  settings page.
- **Download attribution data**: export the data behind the attribution chart in
  Acquisition as a CSV file.
- **Bar and bubble views** for channel performance in Acquisition.
- **Profitability by store** (Rolling out): set profitability values separately
  for each store, and see a profitability column on the Campaigns table.
- **Last-year comparisons and drift insights** (Rolling out): new insight cards
  that compare a day against the same period last year, and cards for metrics
  that drift away from last year's pace.
- **Journeys** (Early access): **Attribution › Journeys** shows the paths
  customers take before they buy, with an Overview tab and a profile for each
  customer.

### Changed

- **Insights redesign** (Rolling out): new feed cards and a detail page for each
  insight. The detail page has a root-cause story, a comparison with last year's
  actual, a feedback box, and the name of the person who raised the insight.
- **UTM Health** has a new page layout with a **What to fix** list: one row for
  each check that needs attention, with the orders and revenue it affects.

### Fixed

- A percentage target typed as `2.4` is now saved as 2.4%. Before, the value
  was scaled wrongly.

## October 2026

### Added

- **Getting started checklist** on Home: setup steps grouped into cards, a setup
  window you open from the checklist, **Mark as done**, and a warning when a data
  source isn't up to date. See [Onboarding](/get-started/onboarding/).
- **14-day trial**: a new workspace gets every feature for 14 days from the
  moment it connects a store. After that it moves to the Free plan unless you
  pick a plan.
- **Setup emails**: after you connect Shopify, an email with your first findings
  arrives within minutes. Later emails suggest data sources and settings worth
  adding.
- **Single sign-on**: sign in through Okta (OIDC). Contact us to set up an SSO
  connection for your workspace.
- **UTM Health**: a page that scores how well your orders are tagged with UTMs,
  plus **UTM Score** settings for weighting each check. Available to every
  workspace.
- **Products in Acquisition**: a Products tab that splits campaign spend down to
  the products it sold, with new and returning customer splits and a drill-down
  from a product to its campaigns.
- **Weekly insights** (Rolling out): weekly insight cards next to the daily ones.
- **Insight feedback** (Rolling out): rate an insight card, and use **Assign to
  me** to take it on.
- **Funnel tab** on an insight (Rolling out).
- **Compare a breakdown with an earlier period**: a metric's dimension breakdown
  can now be compared against a previous period, with totals for each period.
- **Forecast band toggle** on a metric's detail page.

### Changed

- **Report settings** are rebuilt as step cards, with Delivery first. See
  [Configure and schedule daily reports](/daily-reports/configure-and-schedule-daily-reports/).
- **Connecting a store is one step**, with a clear notice if the store is
  already connected.
- **Sign-in** opens with the form ready and Google sign-in first.
- **Performance Max** campaigns now show their own ad sets and creatives in
  Acquisition instead of collapsing into one row.
- **Age and Recency** in Acquisition describe the campaign, ad set or creative
  itself, not the selected date range, so an ad that's still running no longer
  looks dormant.
- **Days without data** now show as missing instead of zero.
- **Store time zones**: daily fulfillment and session figures now follow each
  store's own time zone instead of UTC.
- **Email metrics by store**: Klaviyo email metrics can be filtered by store.
- **Workspace renames** keep old links working.
- **Insight titles** (Rolling out) lead with a verdict, for example "Conversion
  Rate came in better than expected, 13.93% above".

### Fixed

- A store with no session data no longer blanks the whole Shopify dashboard.
- Shopify connection errors now say what went wrong, and reinstalling the
  Shopify app reconnects the store properly.
- Klaviyo figures were double-counted for some connected accounts.

### Removed

- The Customer data platform item in Getting started.

## September 2026

### Added

- **Metric targets**: set a target on a metric and track your pace against it on
  the metric's chart, with the ideal pace and the gap. See
  [Set a metric target](/metric-targets/set-a-metric-target/).
- **Trend alerts** (Rolling out): get alerted when a metric moves outside its
  forecast. See [Set a trend alert](/metric-targets/set-a-trend-alert/).
- **Insights tab** (Rolling out): a feed of notable changes in your metrics. Each
  insight has an AI-written summary, the gap to the forecast, and a root-cause
  breakdown you can drill into. See
  [Read an AI trend insight](/metric-targets/read-an-ai-trend-insight/).
- **Acquisition Explorer** (Rolling out, beta).
- **Several connections per data source**, for example two Meta logins feeding
  one workspace.
- **Data source status on Home**, with a notice to reconnect when a source stops
  sending data.
- **Workspace time zone and currency** in the workspace profile. See
  [Set workspace timezone and currency](/account-management/set-workspace-timezone-and-currency/).
- **Daily report templates by role**: start your daily report from a template
  for your role, such as Founder. See
  [Configure and schedule daily reports](/daily-reports/configure-and-schedule-daily-reports/).

### Changed

- **Simpler sign-up**: no plan picker during onboarding, fewer steps, and the
  workspace is named for you.
- **Insights** (Rolling out) show only the insights in the selected date range,
  and can be filtered by category and pillar.
- **Clearer sign-in errors**, including for expired or invalid email sign-in
  links.
- A new store with no orders yet now says so instead of looking broken.

### Fixed

- **Workspaces with several Shopify stores**: every store is now imported, and a
  day's data is refreshed one store at a time, so refreshing one store no longer
  overwrites another.
- GA4 figures now land on the correct day.
- Renaming a campaign no longer splits its spend and its conversions into two
  rows.
- Insight cards show the date they describe, not the date you opened them.

## August 2026

### Added

- **Shopify App Store**: install Consequential from the Shopify App Store and
  finish setup inside your Shopify admin. See
  [Install the Shopify app](/get-started/install-shopify-app/).
- **Email sign-in links**: sign in with a one-time link sent to your email. No
  password needed.
- **Billing page** in Settings, with a self-serve upgrade.
- **New look**: refreshed light and dark themes.
- **Metric detail page**: compare several metrics on one chart, switch the
  breakdown chart, see a breakdown table by dimension, and export the data. See
  [Explore a metric](/metrics-explorer/explore-a-metric/).
- **Metrics table view**: the table shows Date, Current, Previous and Change even
  when no dimension is picked.
- **Day over Day** comparison for metrics.
- **Store and view picker**: switch between stores and saved views from one
  selector, with a country flag per store. Your choice is remembered.
- **Store currency** and **workspace logo** settings.
- **Data source readiness on Home**: see which sources are ready and which are
  still importing.
- **Partner API**. See
  [Partner integration setup](/integrations-platform-setup/partner-integrations/partner-integration-setup/)
  and the [API reference](/api/).
- When connecting ad accounts, the account list is searchable, and you see which
  Klaviyo role you need before you connect.

### Changed

- **Channel classification** is more accurate. Some orders moved between Direct,
  Organic, Paid and Referral as a result.
- **Dates follow your workspace's day**: date presets and ranges use the
  workspace's time zone rather than your device's, and the range you pick stays
  as you move between pages.
- A metric whose data source has stopped sending now says so, instead of "No
  data for this period".

### Fixed

- Money metrics stopped two days early for workspaces in some currencies.
- Charts that are still loading show a warming state and retry, instead of
  promising a rebuild that wasn't happening.

## July 2026

### Added

- **app.consequential.io**: the Consequential app went live, along with the
  **MCP server** at `mcp.consequential.io`, which lets AI assistants such as
  Claude read your workspace's data once you approve access.
- **Connect your marketing data**: Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, GA4 and
  Klaviyo, from inside the app. See [Connect your data](/get-started/connect-your-data/).
- **Pinned metrics** on the Metrics tab and Home, and a settings page to choose
  and arrange your metrics. See [Update my metrics](/account-management/update-my-metrics/).
- **Scores & grading** and **Badges** settings. See
  [Configure health scores and grading](/account-management/configure-health-scores-and-grading/)
  and [Configure performance badges](/account-management/configure-performance-badges/).
- **Acquisition**: filter every tab by ad platform and creative type; compare
  periods on Channels, Campaigns, Ad sets and Creatives; sparklines, totals and
  CSV export on Channels; sort by health; new versus returning customer
  comparisons; creative detail tabs and the Pages tab. See
  [Understand the Acquisition dashboard](/acquisition-dashboard/understand-the-acquisition-dashboard/).

### Changed

- Grades use one color scheme everywhere: A is green, B blue, C amber and D red.

### Fixed

- Metric tiles that are still warming up retry on their own instead of showing
  an error.
