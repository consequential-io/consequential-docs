Here's what's changed in Consequential, newest first. Each update is listed under
the month it reached [app.consequential.io](https://app.consequential.io).

<Aside>
We switch some features on one workspace at a time. If something here is marked
**Rolling out** or **Early access** and you don't see it yet, ask your
Consequential contact about turning it on.
</Aside>

## November 2026

This month was about asking questions of your data and keeping your team in the
loop.

### New

- **Ask about your metrics** (Rolling out). Ask a question about any metric from
  the Insights tab, or select **Research it** on an insight card. You'll get an
  answer with a chart of the period you asked about and its forecast band.
- **Notes on charts.** Did something happen on certain days, like a promotion or
  a site outage? Add a note against those dates under a metric or insight chart,
  so your team knows why the numbers moved.
- **Pick your display currency.** Show the figures on Acquisition and Metrics in
  any of nine currencies.
- **Custom MER and Custom NC MER.** Two optional blended metrics that don't rely
  on attribution. Switch them on from the new **Settings › Attribution** page.
- **Download attribution data.** Export the numbers behind the attribution chart
  in Acquisition as a CSV.
- **Bar and bubble views** for channel performance in Acquisition.
- **Profitability by store** (Rolling out). Set profitability values for each
  store separately, and see profitability as a column on the Campaigns table.
- **Last-year comparisons and drift insights** (Rolling out). New insight cards
  compare a day with the same period last year, and flag metrics that are
  drifting away from last year's pace.
- **Journeys** (Early access). See the paths customers take before they buy
  under **Attribution › Journeys**, with an Overview tab and a profile for each
  customer.

### Improved

- **Insights got a redesign** (Rolling out). The feed cards are new, and every
  insight now has its own page showing what caused it, how it compares with last
  year, a feedback box, and who raised it.
- **UTM Health** has a new layout with a **What to fix** list: one row for each
  check that needs attention, with the orders and revenue it affects.

### Fixed

- Typing `2.4` for a percentage target now saves it as 2.4%. Before, the value
  was scaled wrongly.

## October 2026

October was about getting new workspaces up and running faster. It also brought
UTM Health and a Products view in Acquisition.

### New

- **A Getting started checklist on Home.** Setup steps are grouped into cards.
  You can open each step from the checklist and mark it done, and you'll see a
  warning if a data source falls behind. See [Onboarding](/get-started/onboarding/).
- **A 14-day trial.** New workspaces get every feature for 14 days, starting when
  they connect a store. After that, they move to the Free plan unless you pick a
  plan.
- **Setup emails.** Within minutes of connecting Shopify, you'll get an email with
  your first findings. Follow-up emails suggest data sources and settings worth
  adding.
- **Single sign-on.** Sign in through Okta (OIDC). Get in touch and we'll set up
  the SSO connection for your workspace.
- **UTM Health.** A page that scores how well your orders are tagged with UTMs,
  plus **UTM Score** settings so you decide how much each check counts. It's
  available in every workspace.
- **Products in Acquisition.** A new Products tab splits campaign spend down to
  the products it sold, with new and returning customer splits. Click a product
  to see the campaigns behind it.
- **Weekly insights** (Rolling out). Weekly insight cards now sit alongside the
  daily ones.
- **Feedback on insights** (Rolling out). Rate an insight card, and use **Assign
  to me** to take it on.
- **A Funnel tab** on insights (Rolling out).
- **Compare breakdowns over time.** Compare a metric's breakdown by dimension
  against an earlier period, with totals for each period.
- **A forecast band toggle** on a metric's detail page.

### Improved

- **Report settings** are rebuilt as step cards, with Delivery first. See
  [Configure and schedule daily reports](/daily-reports/configure-and-schedule-daily-reports/).
- **Connecting a store takes one step**, and you'll see a clear notice if it's
  already connected.
- **Signing in is quicker.** The form is ready as soon as the page opens, with
  Google sign-in first.
- **Performance Max** campaigns now show their own ad sets and creatives in
  Acquisition, instead of collapsing into one row.
- **Age and Recency** in Acquisition now describe the campaign, ad set or
  creative itself rather than the date range you picked, so an ad that's still
  running no longer looks dormant.
- **Days without data** now show as missing rather than as zero.
- **Store time zones.** Daily fulfillment and session numbers follow each store's
  own time zone instead of UTC.
- **Email metrics by store.** You can now filter Klaviyo email metrics by store.
- **Renaming a workspace** no longer breaks old links.
- **Insight titles** (Rolling out) lead with the verdict, like "Conversion Rate
  came in better than expected, 13.93% above".

### Fixed

- A store with no session data no longer blanks out the whole Shopify dashboard.
- Shopify connection errors now tell you what went wrong, and reinstalling the
  Shopify app reconnects your store properly.
- Klaviyo numbers were being counted twice for some connected accounts.

### Removed

- The Customer data platform item in Getting started.

## September 2026

September brought metric targets and the first version of Insights.

### New

- **Metric targets.** Set a target for a metric and see how you're pacing on its
  chart, including the ideal pace and how far off you are. See
  [Set a metric target](/metric-targets/set-a-metric-target/).
- **Trend alerts** (Rolling out). Get an alert when a metric moves outside its
  forecast. See [Set a trend alert](/metric-targets/set-a-trend-alert/).
- **The Insights tab** (Rolling out). A feed of the notable changes in your
  metrics. Each insight comes with an AI-written summary, how far it is from the
  forecast, and a root-cause breakdown you can dig into. See
  [Read an AI trend insight](/metric-targets/read-an-ai-trend-insight/).
- **Acquisition Explorer** (Rolling out, beta).
- **More than one connection per data source.** For example, two Meta logins can
  feed the same workspace.
- **Data source status on Home.** If a source stops sending data, you'll see a
  notice asking you to reconnect it.
- **Workspace time zone and currency** in your workspace profile. See
  [Set workspace timezone and currency](/account-management/set-workspace-timezone-and-currency/).
- **Daily report templates by role.** Start your daily report from a template
  made for your role, such as Founder. See
  [Configure and schedule daily reports](/daily-reports/configure-and-schedule-daily-reports/).

### Improved

- **Signing up is simpler.** There's no plan picker during onboarding, there are
  fewer steps, and we name the workspace for you.
- **Insights** (Rolling out) now show only what falls in the date range you
  picked, and you can filter them by category and pillar.
- **Sign-in errors are clearer**, including when an email sign-in link has
  expired or isn't valid.
- A new store with no orders yet now tells you so, instead of looking broken.

### Fixed

- If you run several Shopify stores, every store is now imported, and each
  store's data refreshes on its own, so one store can no longer overwrite
  another's.
- GA4 numbers now land on the right day.
- Renaming a campaign no longer splits its spend and conversions into two rows.
- Insight cards show the date they're about, not the date you opened them.

## August 2026

August is when Consequential arrived on the Shopify App Store, along with a new
look and a much richer metric page.

### New

- **Shopify App Store.** Install Consequential from the Shopify App Store and
  finish setup without leaving your Shopify admin. See
  [Install the Shopify app](/get-started/install-shopify-app/).
- **Email sign-in links.** Sign in with a one-time link sent to your inbox. No
  password to remember.
- **A Billing page** in Settings, where you can upgrade on your own.
- **A new look**, with refreshed light and dark themes.
- **A richer metric page.** Compare several metrics on one chart, switch the
  breakdown chart, see a breakdown table by dimension, and export the data. See
  [Explore a metric](/metrics-explorer/explore-a-metric/).
- **Metrics table view.** The table shows Date, Current, Previous and Change, even
  when you haven't picked a dimension.
- **Day over Day** comparisons for metrics.
- **One picker for stores and views.** Switch between stores and saved views from
  a single selector, with a country flag for each store. We remember your last
  choice.
- **Store currency** and **workspace logo** settings.
- **Data source readiness on Home**, so you can see which sources are ready and
  which are still importing.
- **Partner API.** See
  [Partner integration setup](/integrations-platform-setup/partner-integrations/partner-integration-setup/)
  and the [API reference](/api/).
- **Easier account connections.** The account list is searchable, and we tell you
  which Klaviyo role you need before you connect.

### Improved

- **Channel classification is more accurate.** As a result, some orders moved
  between Direct, Organic, Paid and Referral.
- **Dates follow your workspace.** Date presets and ranges use your workspace's
  time zone rather than your device's, and the range you pick stays put as you
  move between pages.
- If a metric's data source stops sending data, the metric now says so instead of
  showing "No data for this period".

### Fixed

- Money metrics stopped two days short for workspaces in some currencies.
- Charts that are still loading now show that they're warming up and try again,
  instead of promising a rebuild that wasn't happening.

## July 2026

July is when the Consequential app went live.

### New

- **app.consequential.io is live**, along with our **MCP server** at
  `mcp.consequential.io`. Once you approve access, AI assistants like Claude can
  read your workspace's data.
- **Connect your marketing data.** Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, GA4 and
  Klaviyo, all from inside the app. See [Connect your data](/get-started/connect-your-data/).
- **Pinned metrics** on the Metrics tab and Home, plus a settings page to choose
  and arrange your metrics. See [Update my metrics](/account-management/update-my-metrics/).
- **Scores & grading** and **Badges** settings. See
  [Configure health scores and grading](/account-management/configure-health-scores-and-grading/)
  and [Configure performance badges](/account-management/configure-performance-badges/).
- **A fuller Acquisition dashboard.** Filter every tab by ad platform and creative
  type, compare periods on Channels, Campaigns, Ad sets and Creatives, sort by
  health, and compare new and returning customers. Channels also gets sparklines,
  totals and CSV export, plus there are creative detail tabs and the Pages tab.
  See [Understand the Acquisition dashboard](/acquisition-dashboard/understand-the-acquisition-dashboard/).

### Improved

- Grades use the same colors everywhere: A is green, B blue, C amber and D red.

### Fixed

- Metric tiles that are still warming up now retry on their own instead of
  showing an error.
