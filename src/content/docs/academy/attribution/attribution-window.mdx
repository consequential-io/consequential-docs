Out of the Blue offers flexible attribution windows that help you understand how your marketing touchpoints influence conversions over time.

Attribution windows are especially valuable for teams making data-driven decisions around ad spend, ROAS, CAC, LTV, and any conversion-based KPI.

## What is an attribution window?

An attribution window represents the length of time in which a marketing touchpoint (such as a click or view) can receive credit for a conversion.

For example, with a 7-day window, any conversion that happens within 7 days after the touchpoint may be attributed to that touchpoint.

<Aside type="note">
Attribution windows are available only when you use Accrual Accounting Mode inside Out of the Blue.
</Aside>

## Available attribution windows

The available windows depend on the attribution model you use.

### Click-based attribution windows

For all click models except Clicks + Modeled Views:

- 1-Day Click
- 3-Day Click
- 7-Day Click
- 14-Day Click
- 30-Day Click
- 90-Day Click
- LTV (infinite window)

### Clicks + Modeled Views attribution windows

- 1-Day Click / 1-Day View
- 3-Day Click / 1-Day View
- 7-Day Click / 1-Day View
- 14-Day Click / 1-Day View
- 30-Day Click / 1-Day View
- 90-Day Click / 1-Day View

## Where attribution windows apply

Attribution windows apply across all conversion-based metrics, including:

- Revenue
- Transactions
- Email Sign-Ups
- Subscription Orders
- Custom Events

They also extend to derived metrics, such as:

- ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)
- CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)

Metrics using a window display a "(d)" indicator, such as:

- Attributed Revenue (1d)
- ROAS (7d)
- CAC (3d)
- LTV Attributed Revenue
- LTV ROAS

## Attribution windows glossary

| Attribution window | Meaning | Definition |
|--------------------|---------|------------|
| (1d) | 1-Day Window | Credit given for conversions ≤ 1 day after the touchpoint |
| (3d) | 3-Day Window | Credit given for conversions ≤ 3 days after the touchpoint |
| (7d) | 7-Day Window | Credit given for conversions ≤ 7 days after the touchpoint |
| (14d) | 14-Day Window | Credit given for conversions ≤ 14 days after the touchpoint |
| (30d) | 30-Day Window | Credit given for conversions ≤ 30 days after the touchpoint |
| (60d) | 60-Day Window | Credit given for conversions ≤ 60 days after the touchpoint |
| (90d) | 90-Day Window | Credit given for conversions ≤ 90 days after the touchpoint |
| LTV | Infinite Window | Credit from any conversion after the touchpoint |

## Attribution window examples

This example uses a Linear (equal-weighting) model in Accrual Accounting Mode.

Customer journey:

- Jan 1: Facebook ad click
- Jan 3: Google ad click
- Jan 5: Direct visit, purchase of $90

Because this uses Accrual Mode, each touchpoint only receives credit on the day it occurred.

### Jan 1 performance (Facebook)

| Platform | 1d | 3d | 7d | LTV |
|----------|----|----|----|-----|
| Facebook | $0 | $0 | $30 | $30 |
| Google | $0 | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| Direct | $0 | $0 | $0 | $0 |

The purchase occurred 4 days after the Facebook click, so the 1-day and 3-day windows don't qualify. The 7-day and LTV windows qualify.

### Jan 3 performance (Google)

| Platform | 1d | 3d | 7d | LTV |
|----------|----|----|----|-----|
| Facebook | $0 | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| Google | $0 | $30 | $30 | $30 |
| Direct | $0 | $0 | $0 | $0 |

The purchase occurred 2 days after the Google click, so the 1-day window doesn't qualify. The 3-day, 7-day, and LTV windows qualify.

### Jan 5 performance (Direct)

| Platform | 1d | 3d | 7d | LTV |
|----------|----|----|----|-----|
| Facebook | $0 | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| Google | $0 | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| Direct | $30 | $30 | $30 | $30 |

The purchase occurred the same day as the direct visit, so all windows qualify (within 24 hours).

### Jan 1 to 5 cumulative view

| Platform | 1d | 3d | 7d | LTV |
|----------|----|----|----|-----|
| Facebook | $0 | $0 | $30 | $30 |
| Google | $0 | $30 | $30 | $30 |
| Direct | $30 | $30 | $30 | $30 |

This shows how longer windows attribute more credit to earlier touchpoints, while shorter windows emphasize more recent interactions.

## How to use this as a marketer

Attribution windows let you answer key questions.

### Short windows (1d to 3d)

Ideal for channels with fast purchase cycles, such as:

- Paid social retargeting
- Email
- High-intent performance ads

### Long windows (14d, 30d, 90d)

Ideal for slower-converting prospecting channels, such as:

- Prospecting ads
- YouTube
- Display campaigns

### LTV windows (infinite)

Useful for:

- Subscription businesses
- High-margin products
- Cohort retention strategies
