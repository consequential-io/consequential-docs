The Attribution Home Page is the central command center for your team. This is where media buyers, channel managers, and anyone involved in performance optimization can understand what's working, what needs improvement, and where to act every day.

## Why this page matters

This page serves as your default view when you use the Out of the Blue Attribution suite. It gives you an instant snapshot of how your marketing ecosystem performs across revenue, acquisition, and funnel activity.

This helps you decide:

- Which platforms drive profitable growth
- Which campaigns or ads need adjustments
- Which assets deserve scaling or optimization
- How customers move across your funnel

## Default setting: Attribution – revenue

When you land on the Attribution Home Page, your primary view shows the following key metrics:

1. **Sales:** Total revenue attributed across channels and touchpoints.
2. **New Customers:** How many first-time buyers your marketing efforts drove.
3. **Returning Customers:** Attribution insights into repeat customer purchases.
4. **Top of Funnel (Demand Capture):** Metrics that reflect how your audience discovers your brand.
5. **Bottom of Funnel (Demand Generation):** Insights on how your lower-funnel efforts convert intent into sales.
6. **Organic & Owned Media:** Performance from non-paid channels tied to your brand.

## Profitability panel (available after 90 days)

In the Profitability section, you see information that becomes available after you complete the 90-day learning period with Out of the Blue, which you must complete to benchmark your data accurately.

Learn more about Profitability Benchmarks in the Profitability documentation.

## Additional tabs for deeper analysis

You also gain access to three other major analytical views:

- **Product Analytics:** Understand performance at the product level: what sells, what drives acquisition, and where margins exist.
- **Orders:** Dive into order-level data for clarity on customer journeys and revenue patterns.
- **Creative Analytics:** See which creatives drive the most impact, and which need improvement.
