The Sales page is one of the most frequently used pages inside Out of the Blue. It gives you a complete view of how your marketing performs across every channel, so your team can make fast, informed decisions every day.

Whether you are a media buyer, channel manager, or growth operator, this page is where most of your optimization work begins.

## What you can do on the Sales page

As you scroll, you see a performance table and charts showing each of your channels and their key metrics. This view feels familiar, similar to the interface inside your ad platforms, which makes it easy to navigate and act.

You can sort and analyze performance at the following levels:

- Campaign level
- Ad group / Ad set level
- Ad level
- Landing Pages

The core metrics available here include:

- Spend
- Revenue
- ROAS
- Blended ROAS (Platform)
- NC ROAS
- CAT
- Blended CAT
- NC CAT
- CAC
- CTR
- CVR
- Impressions
- Clicks
- Orders
- CPA
- AOV
- Average CPC
- Average CPM
- Blended
- CTR %

You can also customize your table with any additional metrics you have used in the past, or metrics your team prefers. Once added, your chart and table update retroactively.
