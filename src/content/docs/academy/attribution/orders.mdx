The Orders page gives you a clear view into what drives your purchases. Here, you can explore the full customer journey: how many touchpoints it typically takes for a user to convert, and which channels play the biggest role along the way.

This page helps you answer key questions such as:

- Which channels contributed to conversions?
- How many interactions does it take before a customer purchases?
- What are the most common touchpoints for my buyers?

With these insights, you can better optimize your channel strategy and understand the pathways that lead to higher-intent buyers.

## What you'll find on the Orders page

The walkthrough of the Orders page covers:

1. **Timeframe:** Select a custom timeframe to understand customer journeys over a specific period.
2. **Granularity:** Break down the data at a daily, weekly, or monthly level for deeper insights into user behavior.
3. **E-Com Platforms:** Filter by your connected eCommerce platform(s) to narrow order data to the sources that matter.
4. **Ad Platform:** Analyze which marketing platforms contributed most to driving orders.

## Orders page FAQs

**Can I search for an Order ID?**

Yes. Search for an Order ID using the search bar at the top of the chart for quick lookups and deeper investigations.
