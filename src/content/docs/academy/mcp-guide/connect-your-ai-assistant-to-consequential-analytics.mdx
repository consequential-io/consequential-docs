This guide is for Consequential customers who want to use their AI assistant — Claude (claude.ai, Claude Desktop, Claude Code) or ChatGPT — to ask questions about their marketing analytics.

The Consequential Analytics connector — Consequential's own Model Context Protocol (MCP) server — links your assistant to your Consequential workspace. Ask questions in plain language and get live answers from the same data your dashboards use. Access is **read-only** and scoped to the workspaces your Consequential account is authorized for.

This is Consequential's MCP server (`https://mcp.consequential.io/mcp`) — not a general-purpose MCP server, and unrelated to similarly named products such as Customer.io.

## Before you begin

You need:

- An active Consequential account — the same one you use to sign in at [app.consequential.io](https://app.consequential.io) — with access to at least one workspace.
- One of the supported assistants below.

Every setup uses the same MCP server URL:

```
https://mcp.consequential.io/mcp
```

<Aside type="note">
Not a Consequential customer yet? Visit [consequential.io/support](https://consequential.io/support) to get in touch.
</Aside>

## Set up in claude.ai (web)

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings → Connectors**.
2. Click the **Add custom connector** button.
3. Enter a name (for example, `Consequential Analytics`) and the server URL above, then click **Add**.
4. Click **Connect**. Your browser opens the Consequential sign-in page — sign in with your usual Consequential credentials.
5. Review the consent screen and click **Approve**.

</Steps>

In any chat, Consequential Analytics now appears in the tools menu (the search-and-tools icon in the composer). Enable it for the conversation, then ask your question.

## Set up in Claude Desktop

Claude Desktop shares the same connectors as claude.ai. Follow the claude.ai steps described under **Settings → Connectors**, and the connector becomes available in the desktop app once connected.

## Set up in Claude Code

Run the following command from a terminal:

```bash
claude mcp add --transport http consequential-analytics https://mcp.consequential.io/mcp
```

Then start a Claude Code session and run `/mcp`, select **consequential-analytics**, and choose **Authenticate**. Your browser opens the Consequential sign-in and consent flow. Once you approve, the tools are available in your session.

## Set up in ChatGPT

Custom connectors in ChatGPT require developer mode. To enable it, open **Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced settings** and turn on the **Developer mode** toggle.

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings → Apps & Connectors → Create** (or **Add connector**).
2. Enter a name and the server URL above, select **OAuth** authentication, and save.
3. ChatGPT redirects you to the Consequential sign-in page — sign in and approve the connection.
4. In a chat, enable the connector from the tools menu and ask a question. ChatGPT can also surface Consequential results in its search panel.

</Steps>

## Signing in and permissions

When you connect, you sign in with your existing Consequential account and approve a consent screen. The connection:

- is **read-only** — your assistant can query analytics, never change anything;
- sees only the workspaces your account is a member of — the same access you have in the Consequential app;
- stays signed in automatically (short-lived access tokens are refreshed behind the scenes);
- can be revoked at any time from your assistant's connector settings. Reconnecting later repeats the sign-in and consent flow.

## What you can ask

Once connected, your assistant can:

- **Discover your workspaces** — which workspaces and stores you have access to, with their currency and timezone.
- **Browse your metric catalog** — every metric available to your workspace, with formats and available breakdowns.
- **Query business metrics** — revenue, orders, AOV, customer mix, and more, over any period, with breakdowns, filters, and period-over-period comparisons.
- **Analyze acquisition performance** — channels, campaigns, ad sets, creatives, and landing pages, under platform-reported or attributed models (last-click, first-click).
- **Pull daily history** — day-by-day grades and metrics for any campaign, ad set, or creative.
- **Check service health** — confirm the connection and data service are up.

Example prompts:

> What was my revenue and AOV over the last 30 days compared to the previous period?

> Which campaigns had the best ROAS last week under last-click attribution?

> Break down last month's orders by channel and show me the top landing pages.

> Show me the daily performance history for my top Meta campaign this quarter.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| **"Connection expired" or authentication errors** | Disconnect and reconnect the connector from your assistant's settings. You are asked to sign in again. |
| **`TENANT_NOT_ALLOWED`** | You asked about a workspace your account isn't a member of. Ask your workspace admin to invite you at [app.consequential.io](https://app.consequential.io). |
| **`CORE_UNAVAILABLE`** | The data service is briefly unreachable. Ask your assistant to check the service status, then retry in a minute. |
| **Acquisition numbers look stale** | Acquisition views can lag up to 24 hours after a data refresh. Metric queries are unaffected. |
| **Browser doesn't open for login** | Start a new conversation — the sign-in prompt appears on first tool use. In Claude Code, run `claude mcp list` to confirm the server is registered. Check that your firewall or browser isn't blocking `https://mcp.consequential.io`. |
| **Stuck on the sign-in page, or redirected to the Consequential app instead of back to your assistant** | Close the browser tab and try connecting again from your assistant — this is usually a one-off timed-out redirect. |
| **"No data returned" or unexpected results** | Check that your date range actually has data, and that you're asking about a metric your store tracks. Ask your assistant to show the query it ran to see what was requested. |
| **Anything else** | Contact us at [consequential.io/support](https://consequential.io/support). |

### Collecting diagnostic logs

If you're using Claude Desktop and the steps above don't resolve the issue, these logs are useful to include when contacting support:

| File | Location (macOS) | Location (Windows) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `mcp.log` | `~/Library/Logs/Claude/` | `%APPDATA%\Claude\logs\` |
| `mcp-server-consequential-analytics.log`* | `~/Library/Logs/Claude/` | `%APPDATA%\Claude\logs\` |

\* Named after the connector's identifier (lowercase, hyphenated) rather than its display name — for example, a connector named "Consequential Analytics" typically logs to a file like `mcp-server-consequential-analytics.log`.

## Security and data handling

The connector talks only to Consequential's own backend (a first-party API). It accesses the analytics of workspaces the signed-in user is authorized for, plus the user's account email for authorization. It is read-only, and no data is retained by the connector beyond the OAuth connection records (purged automatically after expiry) and standard service logs. For full details, see the [Consequential privacy policy](https://consequential.io/privacy).
