---
title: CTR (Click-Through Rate)
description: Definition and formula for Click-Through Rate (CTR).
sidebar:
  order: 19
---

Click-Through Rate (CTR) is a digital marketing metric that measures the percentage of people who click on a link, ad, or call-to-action (CTA) out of the total number of people who viewed it.

It helps assess how compelling your ad copy, email subject line, or CTA is to your audience.

```text
CTR = Number of Clicks / Number of Impressions × 100
```
