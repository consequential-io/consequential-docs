---
title: MER - Marketing Efficiency Ratio
description: Definition and formula for the Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER).
sidebar:
  order: 28
---

MER (Marketing Efficiency Ratio) is a marketing metric that measures the net sales generated for every dollar spent on advertising. It gives you a blended, account-wide view of advertising efficiency across all channels rather than the performance of any single campaign.

```text
MER = Net Sales / Effective Ad Spend
```

Effective ad spend is your actual ad spend when it's available, and an estimate based on net sales and your ad spend percentage when it's not.

MER appears on the Metrics page only when your tenant has profitability configured — that is, when both a contribution margin percentage and an ad spend percentage are set.
