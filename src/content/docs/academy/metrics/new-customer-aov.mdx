---
title: New Customer AOV
description: Definition and formula for New Customer Average Order Value.
sidebar:
  order: 22
---

New Customer AOV (Average Order Value) measures the average amount spent per order by first-time (new) customers during a specific time. It helps you understand the purchasing behavior and value of new customers.

```text
New Customer AOV = Total Revenue from New Customers / Number of Orders from New Customers
```
