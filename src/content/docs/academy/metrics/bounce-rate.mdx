---
title: Bounce Rate
description: Definition and formula for the Bounce Rate metric.
sidebar:
  order: 4
---

Bounce Rate is the percentage of users who visit a single page on your website and leave before taking any action. A high bounce rate (usually higher than 57%) means your site isn't giving a good first impression. A user may bounce because of poor design, unmet expectations or slow page-loading time.

```text
Bounce Rate = Single - Page Sessions / Total Sessions × 100
```
