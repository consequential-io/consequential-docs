---
title: CPM (Cost Per Mille / Cost Per 1,000 Impressions)
description: Definition and formula for Cost Per Mille (CPM).
sidebar:
  order: 20
---

CPM stands for Cost Per Mille, where mille is Latin for thousand. It represents the cost of 1,000 ad impressions, or how much you pay for your ad to show 1,000 times — regardless of whether anyone clicks on it.

```text
CPM = (Total Ad Spend) / Total Impressions × 1000
```
