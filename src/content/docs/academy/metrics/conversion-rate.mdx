title: Conversion Rate

sidebar:

CVR (Conversion Rate) is the percentage of visitors to a website who complete a desired action, such as making a purchase, signing up, or downloading something. This metric focuses on individual visitors rather than sessions, measuring how many unique users convert out of the total number of visitors.

```text
Conversion Rate = Total Buyers / Total Visitors
```
