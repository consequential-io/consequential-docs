Total sales are the total revenue from product sales after deducting all costs, including cost of goods, shipping costs, payment gateway costs, handling fees, and taxes.

```text
Total Sales = Order Revenue - (Refund Money - Refunded Tax)
```

<Aside type="note">
Shopify uses the following definitions for its Sales reports, which may cause confusion when comparing sales between your Triple Whale and Shopify dashboard:

- Gross Sales = Total Units Sold * Original Sales Price
- Net Sales = Gross Sales - Discounts - Allowances - Returns
</Aside>
