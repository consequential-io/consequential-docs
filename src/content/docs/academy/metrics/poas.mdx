POAS (Profit on Ad Spend) is a marketing metric that measures the gross profit generated for every dollar spent on advertising. Unlike ROAS, which counts revenue, POAS applies your contribution margin so you see the profitability of your ad campaigns rather than just their top-line return.

```text
POAS = (Net Sales × Contribution Margin % − Effective Ad Spend) / Effective Ad Spend
```

Effective ad spend is your actual ad spend when it's available, and an estimate based on net sales and your ad spend percentage when it's not.

POAS appears on the Metrics page only when your tenant has profitability configured — that is, when both a contribution margin percentage and an ad spend percentage are set.
