---
title: CVR (Sessions)
description: Definition and formula for Conversion Rate by Sessions.
sidebar:
  order: 12
---

CVR (Conversion Rate by Sessions) is the percentage of website sessions that result in a conversion (such as a purchase, sign-up, or download). Instead of measuring individual users, it focuses on sessions — meaning each visit to the site, regardless of whether it's from a new or returning visitor.

```text
CVR (Sessions) = Total Buyers / Total Sessions
```
