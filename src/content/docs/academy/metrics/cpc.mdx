Cost Per Click (CPC) is a digital advertising metric that shows how much you pay each time someone clicks on your ad. It's commonly used in pay-per-click (PPC) advertising platforms like Google Ads, Facebook Ads, LinkedIn Ads, etc.

```text
CPC = Total Ad Spend / Total Clicks
```
