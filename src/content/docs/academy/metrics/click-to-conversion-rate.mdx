---
title: Click to Conversion Rate
description: Definition and formula for the Click to Conversion Rate metric.
sidebar:
  order: 15
---

Click to Conversion Rate measures the percentage of users who clicked on an ad, email, or link and then completed a desired action, like making a purchase or filling out a form. It helps you understand how well your post-click experience (like landing pages or checkout flow) is performing.

```text
Click to Conversion Rate = Number of Conversions / Number of Clicks × 100
```
