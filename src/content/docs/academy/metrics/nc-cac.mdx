---
title: NC-CAC (New Customer CAC)
description: Definition and formula for New Customer CAC (NC-CAC).
sidebar:
  order: 17
---

A focused version of CAC that isolates new-customer orders in the denominator. Ad spend itself isn't split between new and returning customers — the full spend total is divided by new-customer orders only, so NC-CAC answers "what would it cost per order if this spend only bought new customers."

```text
NC-CAC = Total Ad Spend / New Customer Orders
```
