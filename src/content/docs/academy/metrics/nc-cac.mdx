---
title: NC-CAC (New Customer CAC)
description: Definition and formula for New Customer CAC (NC-CAC).
sidebar:
  order: 17
---

It specifically refers to the cost of acquiring a new customer, instead of repeat customers. It's essentially a focused version of CAC, measuring how much you spend to bring in first-time buyers only.

```text
NC-CAC = Total Spend on New Customer Acquisition / Number of New Customers Acquired
```
