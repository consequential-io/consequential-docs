---
title: ROAS - Return on Ad Spend
description: Definition and formula for Return on Ad Spend (ROAS).
sidebar:
  order: 2
---

ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) is a marketing metric that measures the revenue generated for every dollar spent on advertising. It helps you understand the effectiveness and profitability of your ad campaigns.

```text
ROAS = Revenue from Ads / Cost of Ads
```
