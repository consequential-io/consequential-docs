Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) measures how much ad spend it takes to generate one purchase. It's calculated across all purchases — new and returning customers alike. To see the cost of new customers specifically, use [NC-CAC](/academy/metrics/nc-cac/).

```text
CAC = Total Ad Spend / Total Purchases
```
