Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) is a business metric that represents the total cost of acquiring a new customer. It includes all the expenses associated with marketing, sales, and any other costs directly tied to bringing in new customers.

```text
CAC = Total Sales and Marketing Cost / Number of New Customer Acquired
```
