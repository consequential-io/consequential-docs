---
title: Avg. Discount Amount
description: Definition and formula for the Average Discount Amount metric.
sidebar:
  order: 25
---

Average Discount Amount measures the average value of discounts applied per order or per item over a specific period. It helps you understand how much you're giving away through promotions, coupons, or markdowns.

```text
Avg. Discount Amount = Total Discount Given / Number of Order
```
