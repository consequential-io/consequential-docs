New Customer Cost is another way of referring to Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) — the amount a business spends to acquire one new customer.

It focuses specifically on first-time customers and includes all costs associated with bringing them in.

```text
New Customer Cost = Total Marketing + Sales Spend / Number of New Customer Acquired
```
