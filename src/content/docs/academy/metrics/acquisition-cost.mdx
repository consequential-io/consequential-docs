Acquisition Cost refers to the total amount of money a business spends to acquire a new customer, lead, or user. It's a broader term that can apply to Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) or Lead Acquisition Cost, depending on the goal.

```text
Acquistions Cost = Total Acquistion Spend / Number of Acquistion
```
