---
title: RC-CAC (Returning Customer CAC)
description: Definition and formula for Returning Customer CAC (RC-CAC).
sidebar:
  order: 18
---

It represents the cost of re-engaging or reactivating a previous customer to make another purchase. While CAC typically focuses on acquiring new customers, RC-CAC focuses on the cost of bringing existing customers back, often through email marketing, loyalty programs, retargeting ads, or personalized offers.

```text
RC-CAC = Total Spend on Returning Customer Campaigns / Number of Returning Customer Reconverted
```
