---
title: RC-CAC (Returning Customer CAC)
description: Definition and formula for Returning Customer CAC (RC-CAC).
sidebar:
  order: 18
---

The counterpart to NC-CAC: the same total ad spend divided by returning-customer orders instead of new-customer orders. As with NC-CAC, spend itself isn't split by customer type — only the order count in the denominator changes.

```text
RC-CAC = Total Ad Spend / Returning Customer Orders
```
