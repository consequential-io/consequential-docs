## What Consequential does

Consequential runs a team of AI agents on top of your store, ad and analytics data. They work from
reconciled orders rather than platform-reported numbers, bring each recommendation to your team
with the evidence behind it, and change nothing until you approve it. The same context is
available to Claude and ChatGPT through the [MCP server](/academy/mcp-guide/connect-your-ai-assistant-to-consequential-analytics/).

There is a free plan, and paid plans start at $499 a month. To start, follow the
[Quickstart](/get-started/overview/): connect your store, connect Meta, Google and TikTok, then
open Home for your first read. Short answers to common questions are in
[Common buyer questions](/faq/about/buyer-questions/).
