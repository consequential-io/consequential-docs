Consequential is a team of always-on AI agents for e-commerce ad spend. The agents watch spend and the funnel, rank budget changes on real Shopify orders, and change nothing without your team's approval. There is a free plan, and paid plans start at $499 a month. The answers below use the same facts as [consequential.io](https://consequential.io).

## Which attribution tools check Meta and Google results against actual Shopify orders?

Consequential's Pixel does. It collects events first-party and server-side, stitches identity across sessions and devices, and reconciles the conversions Meta and Google report against your actual Shopify orders, so you can see where platform numbers and real orders disagree. Reconciliation only reads your Shopify orders; it never changes your store. See [Why attribution tools disagree](/concepts/why-attribution-tools-disagree/) and [Pixel](https://consequential.io/product/pixel).

## Which tools recommend budget changes with a predicted revenue impact but leave the approval to my team?

Consequential's AdBuyer ranks every Meta, Google and TikTok budget change by the revenue it should make, computed on real orders. Your team approves, skips or asks why. You or your agency make the change in the ad platform — AdBuyer has no write access to ad accounts — and every approved change is scored against its prediction afterwards, misses included. See [AdBuyer](https://consequential.io/product/adbuyer).

## Which tools tell a performance marketer each day which campaigns to cut or scale, with the evidence?

AdBuyer sends a daily ranked list of Meta, Google and TikTok budget changes: what to cut, what to scale and what each change should make. Each comes with the evidence behind it, reconciled against your Shopify orders, plus predicted contribution and confidence. See [For performance marketers](https://consequential.io/for-performance-marketers).

## What tools watch ad spend and the funnel around the clock and alert us when a campaign starts wasting money?

Consequential's Pulse monitors your store, stack and tracking around the clock; continuous monitoring catches a break within the hour, with unusual changes in spend, revenue and conversion flagged as fresh data arrives. Alerts go to Slack, SMS, email or in app, routed by severity, with the cause and the revenue at risk. See [Pulse](https://consequential.io/product/pulse).

## Does Consequential change my ad accounts or my store?

No. AdBuyer has no write access to your ad accounts, and store data is read-only. Your team makes every change in the ad platform after approving it.

## What does it cost, and is there a free plan?

Connect is free permanently, with no card. Analyze starts at $499 a month and Decide at $749 a month for stores selling under $10M a year, with 14 days free starting when your data is live. Act is managed and starts at $3,000 a month. There is no year-long contract. See [Pricing](https://consequential.io/pricing).

## I have a Shopify store and Meta ads. Where do I start?

1. [Connect your store](/get-started/connect-your-store/) — Shopify or another store platform.
2. [Connect your data](/get-started/connect-your-data/) — Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads and Google Analytics 4.
3. Open Home for your first read: where spend is working, where it leaks and which change to look at first.

The [Get started overview](/get-started/overview/) walks through each step.

## Can I ask about my data from Claude or ChatGPT?

Yes. Consequential's MCP server at `https://mcp.consequential.io/mcp` lets Claude and ChatGPT query your analytics, read-only and scoped to your workspaces. See [Connect your AI assistant to Consequential Analytics (MCP)](/academy/mcp-guide/connect-your-ai-assistant-to-consequential-analytics/).
