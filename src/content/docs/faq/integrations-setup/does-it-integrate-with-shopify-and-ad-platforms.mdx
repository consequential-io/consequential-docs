---
title: Does it integrate with Shopify and ad platforms?
description: The platforms Consequential connects with, and how each connection works.
---

Yes. Consequential connects with Shopify, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Google Analytics 4, TikTok Ads, and Klaviyo.

Shopify is self-serve: connect it yourself from the **Connect your data** step during onboarding, via a standard OAuth approval. The ad and analytics platforms (Meta Ads, Google Ads, GA4, TikTok Ads, Klaviyo) are currently set up by Consequential's team on your behalf rather than connected by you directly — see [Connect your data](/get-started/connect-your-data/) for details.
