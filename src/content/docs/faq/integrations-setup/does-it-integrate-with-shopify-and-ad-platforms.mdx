---
title: Does it integrate with Shopify and ad platforms?
description: The platforms Consequential connects with, and how each connection works.
---

Yes. Consequential connects with Shopify and other store platforms, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Google Analytics 4, TikTok Ads, Klaviyo and more. **Settings → Connectors** lists about 30 sources.

You connect them yourself. Connect Shopify from the **Connect your store** dialog, and your ad, analytics and email platforms from the **Onboarding** card on Home or from **Settings → Connectors**. Sources marked coming soon are set up for you by our team. See [Connect your store](/get-started/connect-your-store/) and [Connect your data](/get-started/connect-your-data/).
