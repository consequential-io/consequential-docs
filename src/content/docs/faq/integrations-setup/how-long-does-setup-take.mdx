---
title: How long does setup take?
description: Setup time for connecting Consequential to your store.
---

Connecting Shopify yourself takes just a few minutes — it's a standard OAuth approval from the **Connect your data** onboarding step. Ad and analytics platforms (Meta Ads, Google Ads, GA4, TikTok Ads, Klaviyo) are set up by Consequential's team on your behalf; timing depends on your onboarding schedule. See [Connect your data](/get-started/connect-your-data/) for details.
