---
title: How long does setup take?
description: Setup time for connecting Consequential to your store.
---

Connecting your store and your ad, analytics and email platforms takes a few minutes for each one. Your data then takes a while to load the first time. Sources marked coming soon are set up by our team, and their timing depends on your onboarding schedule. See [Connect your data](/get-started/connect-your-data/) for details.
