---
title: How does Consequential help improve ROAS?
description: Learn how Consequential gives eCommerce growth teams visibility into the full ad-click-to-purchase journey and prevents signal loss.
---

Consequential gives you visibility into the full journey from ad click to purchase or bounce, helping you improve targeting, signals, and landing page fit in one place. By detecting the technical and tracking issues that waste ad dollars, it prevents signal loss and performance decay — improving the efficiency of every dollar you spend. It's built specifically for eCommerce growth teams trying to scale profitably.
