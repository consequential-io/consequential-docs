A spike or a dip is easier to read when you know what happened that day. Notes let you record that context — a sale, a site release, a tracking change, or an outage — against the days it covered. Everyone on your team sees the note under the chart, so the next person who asks "what happened here?" gets the answer without digging.

You can add notes in two places:

- Under the chart on a [metric's detail view](/metrics-explorer/explore-a-metric/).
- On the **Chart** tab of an insight's detail page. See [Read an AI trend insight](/metric-targets/read-an-ai-trend-insight/).

## Prerequisites

- Access to your Consequential account. Any member of your account can add, edit, and delete notes.
- To add notes on a metric's detail view, a single store selected in the scope switcher. The metric page doesn't show notes while a view, such as **All stores**, is selected. See [Where a note belongs](#where-a-note-belongs).

## Add a note

<Steps>

1. Open a metric from **Metrics** in the sidebar, or open an insight from **Insights** and stay on its **Chart** tab.

2. Scroll to the **Notes** section under the chart and click **Add a note**.

   On an insight's **Chart** tab, you can also click a day on the chart. The add form opens with that day already set as the start and end day.

3. Set the start day and the end day. For a single day, leave both on the same date. The form shows *one day* or *a range* so you can confirm what you picked.

4. In the **What happened?** field, describe the event. For example: *Black Friday sitewide 20% off*.

5. Select the kind of note:

   - **Promotion** — a sale, discount, or campaign push.
   - **Release** — a site or app change that went live.
   - **Tracking change** — a change to pixels, tags, or how data is collected.
   - **Outage** — downtime on your site or on a connected service.
   - **Note** — anything else. This is the default.

6. Click **Save**.

</Steps>

The note appears in the list under the chart, with its dates, a colored badge for its kind, and your text.

### Limits

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Start and end day** | Required. The end day can't be before the start day. |
| **What happened?** | Required. Up to 500 characters. Leading and trailing spaces are removed. |
| **Kind** | One of **Promotion**, **Release**, **Tracking change**, **Outage**, or **Note**. |

**Save** stays disabled until every field meets its rule. Once you've entered text, a message next to the buttons tells you what to fix.

## Where a note belongs

A note belongs to the scope of the chart you write it on. It's either a store note or an account-wide note:

| Where you write it | What kind of note it is | Where it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A metric's detail view, with one store selected | A store note | That store's metric charts and that store's insight cards. |
| An insight card for one store | A store note | That store's metric charts and that store's insight cards. |
| An insight card tagged **All stores** | An account-wide note | Every metric chart and insight card in the account. |

The text under the **Notes** heading tells you which scope you're writing in. A store's chart lists that store's notes together with the account-wide ones.

<Aside type="note">
The metric detail view doesn't show the **Notes** section while a view, such as **All stores**, is selected. A view groups several stores, so a note written there would have no single store to belong to. Switch to one store to add a note. See [Switch stores and views](/how-tos/onboarding/manage-store-preferences/#switch-stores-and-views).
</Aside>

You can't move a note to a different scope after you save it. To change its scope, delete it and add it again from the right chart.

## Which notes the list shows

On a metric's detail view, the list shows the notes whose days overlap the date range in the page header. A note that runs from Nov 27 to Dec 1 appears on any range that includes at least one of those days. Notes are sorted by start day.

On an insight's **Chart** tab, the list shows every note in the card's scope.

## Edit or delete a note

<Steps>

1. Find the note in the **Notes** list.

2. To change it, click **Edit**, update the dates, text, or kind, and click **Save**.

3. To remove it, click **Delete**, and then click **Confirm delete**. Click **Keep** to leave the note in place.

</Steps>

A deleted note disappears from every chart it showed on.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| There's no **Notes** section under the metric chart. | A view is selected. Switch the scope to a single store. |
| **Save** is disabled and no message shows. | The **What happened?** field is empty. Describe the event. |
| *Write what happened* | The **What happened?** field holds only spaces. Describe the event. |
| *The end day cannot be before the start day* | Move the end day to the start day or later. |
| *Could not load notes.* | Refresh the page. If the message stays, contact your Consequential admin or support. |
| A note you expected isn't listed. | Check the date range in the page header and the scope switcher. A store note doesn't show on another store's charts or on account-wide charts. |
