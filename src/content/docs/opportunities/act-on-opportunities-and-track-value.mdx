Consequential reviews your campaigns, discount codes, and landing pages and flags the spots where you could keep money that is leaking today. Each flag is an **opportunity**. When you fix one, you mark it as done and Consequential measures what changed, so the **Value** page shows what you have recovered over time.

<Aside type="note">
Opportunities and Value are rolling out to workspaces gradually. If the **What we found for you** card and the Value page aren't in your workspace yet, they appear once the feature is enabled for your account.
</Aside>

## Prerequisites

- A connected Shopify store and at least one connected ad datasource.
- Consequential's daily data pipeline needs to have run so there is enough history to review your campaigns, discount codes, and landing pages.
- The feature must be enabled for your workspace. Until it is, the Value page and the opportunities list are not available.

## How a recovery works

<Steps>

1. Consequential reviews your last 30 days and lists each opportunity it finds.
2. You act on an opportunity in your store or ad platform — for example, you turn off an unprofitable campaign.
3. You mark the opportunity as done with the **I did this** button.
4. Consequential measures the result over about the next 30 days and records what you recovered on the Value page.

</Steps>

## What Consequential looks for

Each opportunity falls into one of three types:

| Type | What it flags |
| --- | --- |
| **Campaign** | Campaigns that cost more than they return — for example, a campaign whose attributed orders are worth far less than your store average. |
| **Discount code** | Discount codes that cost more than the margin they bring in. |
| **Landing page** | Landing pages that get traffic but don't convert. |

## Review your opportunities

Your email digest links to the **Opportunities** page, where each opportunity appears as its own row. If a row was in one of your emails, it shows the date it was sent.

The **What you could keep** header at the top of the page summarizes the list:

- The total you could keep, based on the last 30 days. This is a single amount or a range.
- **You kept &lt;amount&gt; so far**, once you have recovered value from earlier opportunities.
- A **trial goal** progress bar, if your workspace is on a trial.
- A note on how the total is capped: Consequential counts at most a set share of your ad spend plus a set share of your revenue, so the figure stays conservative.

Each row shows the opportunity type, a short title, a plain-language explanation, and the amount — a single figure for the clearest opportunities, or a range. Small opportunities below the workspace floor are not listed on their own; the page tells you when that is the case.

## Mark an opportunity as done

When you have acted on an opportunity in your store or ad platform:

<Steps>

1. Open the **Opportunities** page or the **Value** page.
2. Find the opportunity you acted on.
3. Select **I did this**.

</Steps>

Consequential confirms with **Marked as done. We measure the result over the next 30 days.** From then on, the opportunity moves from **Open** to **Measuring**, and Consequential tracks whether the change recovered the value it estimated.

<Aside type="caution">
Only mark an opportunity as done after you have made the change. The button starts a 30-day measurement — it does not make the change in your store or ad platform for you.
</Aside>

## Track your recovered value

The home page shows a **What we found for you** card with your headline figures. Select **See your value** to open the Value page.

### What the figures mean

The **What this is worth** section shows up to three figures. A figure is hidden when there is nothing to show for it.

| Figure | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Found so far** | The total value Consequential has found you could keep. |
| **Recovered** | The value you have actually recovered from opportunities you marked as done. |
| **Open now** | The value, per month, still sitting in opportunities you haven't acted on. |

### Everything we found

Below the figures, **Everything we found** lists every opportunity with its current state. Open opportunities keep an **I did this** button so you can mark them from this page too.

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | Found, but not yet acted on. |
| **Measuring** | You marked it done and Consequential is measuring the result. |
| **Recovered** | The change recovered value. |
| **Dismissed** | Set aside and no longer counted. |
| **Snoozed** | Hidden for now and due to return later. |
| **Expired** | No longer current. |
| **Hidden** | Not shown as a standalone opportunity. |

## Your plan

The Value page shows a **Your plan** section based on your current plan:

- **On a trial** — the date your trial ends, with a prompt to keep Consequential so it keeps finding opportunities for you.
- **On the free plan** — a prompt to upgrade so you can act on opportunities with Consequential.
- **On a paid plan** — a summary of how much value you are tracking in open opportunities each month and how much you have recovered so far.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause and resolution |
| --- | --- |
| The Value page or Opportunities page sends you back home | The feature isn't enabled for your workspace yet, or your account doesn't have access. It appears once the feature is enabled for you. |
| **No opportunities yet** | Consequential is still reviewing your campaigns, discount codes, and landing pages. New opportunities appear here as they are found. |
| **Nothing to show yet** on the Value page | Consequential is still reviewing your store. Figures appear once there is something to report. |
| An opportunity won't mark as done | If it is already done, the page tells you so and refreshes. If it failed to save, try again. |
