Yes. It's a next generation revenue observability platform that goes beyond attribution to provide full funnel visibility, anomaly detection, and AI-assisted root cause analysis.

The two tools can report different revenue for the same orders. That divergence is expected — see [Why your attribution tools disagree](/concepts/why-attribution-tools-disagree/) for the reasons and how to compare them correctly.
