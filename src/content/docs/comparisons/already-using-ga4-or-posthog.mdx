Yes. Those tools show what users do, but they often stop at raw metrics like bounce rate, scrolls, and events. Consequential focuses on the why behind performance drops and connects user behavior to campaign targeting and landing page context, helping you isolate whether the issue is the audience, message, or on-site flow in one system.
