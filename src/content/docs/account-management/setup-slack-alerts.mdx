Integrate your Slack workspace with the application to receive alerts directly in Slack.

<Steps>

1. Add your Slack workspace to the application. Click the option to connect Slack.
2. Grant permission to enable the application on your Slack workspace. Click **Allow** to enable this interaction.
3. Set up receiving alerts. Click **On**, then click **Save Changes**. This applies the changes, and you start receiving alerts.

</Steps>

After you set up the application, you receive alerts that provide detailed information about the detected event. Each alert includes the current status, the start time, the provider you chose, what happened, and the actions you can take. To navigate directly to the event, click the links in the alert.
