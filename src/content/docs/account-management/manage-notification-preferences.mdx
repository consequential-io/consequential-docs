Manage which notifications you receive and how they reach you.

{/* TODO: migrate image artifact(s) artifact-13994 from ART-1232 — cannot be downloaded via DevRev MCP */}

<Steps>

1. Go to [outoftheblue.ai](https://outoftheblue.ai) and click **Login**.
2. Navigate to **Account Settings** and select **Preferences**.
3. Click **Notifications**.
4. Select the impact level: **High**, **Medium**, or **Low**.
5. Click **Alert Channels** to choose your notification methods: **Email**, **SMS**, or **Slack**.
6. Update the **Minimum dollar impact to capture** (optional).

</Steps>
