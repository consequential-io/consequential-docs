Manage which notifications you receive and how they reach you.

<Aside type="note">
This page previously described impact-level thresholds (High/Medium/Low), a choice of Email/SMS/Slack alert channels, and a "minimum dollar impact to capture" field. Confirmed directly against the live Settings &gt; Preferences page: none of that exists today. It exposes exactly the on/off toggles listed below — no severity threshold, no SMS/Slack channel, no dollar-impact filter.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. Log in to the Consequential app at [app.consequential.io](https://app.consequential.io).
2. Click **Settings** in the sidebar (bottom left, below Support).
3. Open the **Preferences** tab (the default tab).
4. Under **Email notifications**, toggle **Weekly digest**, **Alerts**, **Invitations**, and **Product updates** on or off.
5. Under **In-app notifications**, toggle **Alerts**, **Mentions**, and **System messages** on or off.
6. Click **Save changes** in the save bar to apply your changes (or **Discard** to undo).

</Steps>
