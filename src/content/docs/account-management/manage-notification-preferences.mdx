Manage which notifications you receive and how they reach you.

<Aside type="caution">
This page previously described impact-level thresholds (High/Medium/Low), a choice of Email/SMS/Slack alert channels, and a "minimum dollar impact to capture" field. Those weren't found in the current Consequential app's Settings &gt; Preferences page — it currently exposes simple on/off toggles for specific notification types (see steps below), with no severity threshold, SMS/Slack channel, or dollar-impact filter. Please verify against the live app before publishing, in case this is a planned feature or lives elsewhere.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. Log in to the Consequential app at [app.consequential.io](https://app.consequential.io).
2. Click your avatar in the sidebar, then click **Settings**.
3. Click **Preferences**.
4. Under **Email notifications**, toggle **Weekly digest**, **Alerts**, **Invitations**, and **Product updates** on or off.
5. Under **In-app notifications**, toggle **Alerts**, **Mentions**, and **System messages** on or off.
6. Click **Save** in the save bar to apply your changes.

</Steps>
