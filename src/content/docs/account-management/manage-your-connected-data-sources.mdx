Consequential turns the data from your store, ad platforms, and analytics tools into spend alerts and performance grades. The **Integrations** page in Settings is where you review every data source connected to your workspace and connect new ones yourself.

This guide shows you how to open the Integrations page, connect a data source, manage the accounts a source syncs, and disconnect a source.

## Prerequisites

- Access to your Consequential account with a store selected in the sidebar store switcher. New connections attach to the active store.
- The **Manager**, **Admin**, or **Owner** role to view the page. Only **Admin** and **Owner** can connect or disconnect sources. Other roles see a read-only view.

<Aside type="note">
The Consequential team labels some sources as **Coming soon** and sets these up for you, so you don't need to take any action.
</Aside>

## Open the Integrations page

<Steps>

1. In Consequential, open **Settings** from the sidebar.
2. Select the **Integrations** tab. The **Data sources** section lists every source that feeds this workspace's metrics.

</Steps>

Each source shows its name, a status badge, and — once connected — the account it's linked to and the date it was connected.

### Connection statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Connected** | The source is linked and syncing data into your workspace. |
| **Disconnected** | The source was connected before but is no longer syncing. Reconnect it to resume. |
| **Available** | The source is ready for you to connect. |
| **Coming soon** | The Consequential team sets this source up for you. |

## Connect a Shopify store

Shopify connects through a hosted approval flow on Shopify's side.

<Steps>

1. Find your Shopify store in the **Data sources** list and click **Connect**.
2. In the **Connect Shopify** dialog, enter your store domain, for example `acme.myshopify.com`.
3. Click **Continue to Shopify**. Consequential sends you to Shopify to approve access.
4. Approve the connection on Shopify. You return to the Integrations page and the store's status changes to **Connected**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
Connecting attaches the store to the store selected in your sidebar switcher. If you manage more than one store, confirm the correct store is active before you connect.
</Aside>

## Connect an ads, analytics, or email source

Ad platforms, analytics tools, and email sources connect through Supermetrics in a popup window.

<Steps>

1. Find the source in the **Data sources** list and click **Connect**. A Supermetrics sign-in window opens.
2. Sign in to the source and approve access. When the window closes, the source's status changes to **Connected**.
3. In the **accounts** dialog that opens, select the accounts you want Consequential to sync, then confirm your selection.

</Steps>

If the connection window expires before you finish, the source stays unconnected. Click **Connect** to start again.

## Manage the accounts a source syncs

For a connected ads, analytics, or email source, you can change which accounts Consequential syncs at any time.

<Steps>

1. Find the connected source in the **Data sources** list.
2. Click **Accounts**.
3. Update your selection in the dialog, then confirm.

</Steps>

## Reconnect a source

A source with a **Disconnected** status is no longer syncing. To resume it, click **Reconnect** and follow the same steps as the first connection.

## Disconnect a source

<Steps>

1. Find the connected source in the **Data sources** list.
2. Click **Disconnect**.
3. Confirm in the dialog.

</Steps>

Disconnecting revokes Consequential's stored credentials for the source and pauses its data sync. Metrics that depend on the source stop updating until you reconnect it.

<Aside type="caution">
Disconnecting stops new data from flowing in. Reconnect the source to resume syncing.
</Aside>
