Add a teammate to your EdgeTag app so they can access your workspace.

<Steps>

1. Log in to [app.edgetag.io](https://app.edgetag.io).
2. Click the burger menu in the top right.
3. Click **Teams**.
4. Click **Add Users**.
5. Add `support@consequential.io` as a user.

</Steps>
