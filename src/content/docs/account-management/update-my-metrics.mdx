Customize the metrics that appear on your Home page.

{/* TODO: migrate image artifact(s) artifact-13984, artifact-13985 from ART-1231 — cannot be downloaded via DevRev MCP */}

<Steps>

1. After login, navigate to **Account Settings** and select **Preferences**.
2. Click **My Metrics**.
3. Update the mandatory fields you want to display on the **Home** page.
4. Click **Save changes**.

</Steps>

Your customized "My Metrics" page is visible under **Metrics Overview** on the **Home** page.
