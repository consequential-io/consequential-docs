<Aside type="caution">
	This page previously described a single "My Metrics" screen under
	Settings → Preferences. That screen doesn't exist — pinning metrics to
	Home and customizing the Metrics page layout are two separate, real
	flows, documented below.
</Aside>

## Pin a metric to Home

<Steps>

1. Open **Metrics** in the sidebar, then **Category**.
2. Find the metric you want on Home, and click its **Pin** button (top
   right of the metric card).
3. Open **Home** — the metric now appears under Pinned metrics.

</Steps>

## Customize the Metrics page layout

This controls the order, visibility, and grouping of metrics on the Metrics
page itself — it doesn't affect Home.

<Steps>

1. From the Metrics page, click the gear icon (**Metrics settings**), or go
   to **Settings** in the sidebar and open the **Metrics** tab directly.
2. Switch between **My layout** (personal to you) and **Tenant default**.
3. Reorder, hide, or regroup metrics within each priority group (P1 Revenue
   & Profitability, P2 Profitable Acquisition, P3 Improve Conversion, P4
   Grow Active Users, P5 LTV Growth).
4. Click **Save changes**. Use **Reset to tenant default** to discard your
   personal layout and fall back to the tenant's.

</Steps>
