Update your profile information in the Out of the Blue app.

{/* TODO: migrate image artifact(s) artifact-13981 from ART-1230 — cannot be downloaded via DevRev MCP */}

<Steps>

1. Go to [outoftheblue.ai](https://outoftheblue.ai).
2. Click the **Login** button to get started.
3. Click **Account Settings**.
4. Under account settings, click **Preferences**.
5. Update the available fields:
   - **Name** — auto-populated from your login profile.
   - **Phone number** — used for SMS alerts.
   - **Email** — can't be edited.
6. Click **Save changes**.

</Steps>
