<Aside type="caution">
This page previously described a "Preferences" screen with just Name, Phone number, and Email fields under an "Account Settings" menu. In the current Consequential app, the closest match is the standalone **Profile** page (opened from the avatar menu), which exposes more fields than that — First name, Last name, Username, Phone, Date of birth, Gender, Department, and Designation — and doesn't include an editable Email field. Please verify this flow against the live app before publishing.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. Log in to the Consequential app at [app.consequential.io](https://app.consequential.io).
2. Click your avatar in the sidebar to open the account menu.
3. Click **Profile**.
4. Update the available fields, such as **First name**, **Last name**, **Phone**, **Username**, **Department**, and **Designation**.
5. Click **Save changes**.

</Steps>
