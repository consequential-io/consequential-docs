Invite colleagues to your Consequential workspace, control what each person can do with roles, and manage invitations that are still pending. You manage everyone who has access to a workspace from the **Team** settings page.

## Prerequisites

- A Consequential workspace at [app.consequential.io](https://app.consequential.io).
- An **Owner**, **Admin**, or **Manager** role in the workspace to view the team.
- An **Owner** or **Admin** role to invite people, change roles, or remove members. Managers can view the team but can't make changes.

## Roles

Every member has one role per workspace. The role controls what the member can do.

<Aside type="caution">
Directly confirmed on the live Team settings page: the Role dropdown defaults to **VIEWER**, and an existing pending invitation used **Admin** — so Admin and Viewer are real. The app went unreachable (local dev environment outage) before the dropdown could be opened to confirm the full list, so **Owner, Manager, and Editor below are not yet independently verified** — they may be accurate, outdated, or incomplete. Re-check against the live Role dropdown before treating this table as final.
</Aside>

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Owner | Full access, including billing and workspace ownership. Assigned to the workspace creator. You can't change this role from the Team page. |
| Admin | Manage workspace settings, integrations, and team members. |
| Manager | View the workspace and its team. Can't change team members or workspace settings. |
| Editor | Work with dashboards and reports. Can't manage the team or workspace settings. |
| Viewer | Read-only access to the workspace. |

<Aside type="note">
You can't assign the **Owner** role from the Team page. Ownership stays with the workspace creator.
</Aside>

## View your team

<Steps>

1. Log in to [app.consequential.io](https://app.consequential.io).
2. Open **Settings**, then select the **Team** tab.
3. Review the **Members** list. Each row shows the member's name, email, role, and when they were last active. Your own row is marked with a **You** badge.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
On the live Team page for a real tenant, the Members list rendered as "No members yet" rather than a populated table, so the exact row columns above (name/email/role/last-active, **You** badge) couldn't be confirmed against real data before the app became unreachable. Re-check against a tenant with members once available.
</Aside>

## Invite people

Send invitations to up to 5 people at a time. Each invitee receives an email with a link to join your workspace.

<Aside type="caution">
The "5 people at a time" limit is not yet independently confirmed — the live page showed an **+ Add another** control without a visible cap before the app became unreachable. Verify the actual limit before relying on this number.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. On the **Team** page, find the **Invite people** section.
2. Enter the invitee's email address in the **Email address** field.
3. Select a role from the **Role** dropdown. You can assign **Admin**, **Manager**, **Editor**, or **Viewer**.
4. To invite more people, add another row and repeat. You can send up to 5 invitations per batch.
5. Select **Send invitations**.

</Steps>

After you send, a confirmation shows the result for each email, such as how many invitations were sent, how many people were already invited, and how many were already members.

<Aside type="tip">
Invitations expire after a set period. If someone's invitation expires before they accept it, revoke it and invite them again.
</Aside>

## Change a member's role

<Steps>

1. On the **Team** page, find the member in the **Members** list.
2. Select a new role from the role dropdown on their row.

</Steps>

The change takes effect immediately. You can't change your own role or the Owner's role.

## Remove a member

Removing a member revokes their access to the workspace.

<Steps>

1. On the **Team** page, find the member in the **Members** list.
2. Select **Remove** on their row.
3. Confirm the removal in the dialog.

</Steps>

You can't remove yourself or the Owner.

## Manage pending invitations

Invitations that have been sent but not yet accepted appear in the **Pending invitations** list, along with the invitee's email, assigned role, who invited them, and the expiry date.

To revoke a pending invitation:

<Steps>

1. On the **Team** page, find the invitation in the **Pending invitations** list.
2. Select **Revoke**.
3. Confirm in the dialog.

</Steps>

Revoking an invitation invalidates its link. To invite the person again, send a new invitation.
