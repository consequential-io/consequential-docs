Setting up the Consequential Pixel (EdgeTag) is like working with any other JS / iOS / Android SDK. Enter your business email and the system walks you through setup step by step. You can add teammates once setup is complete.

<Aside type="note">
This guide covers the Consequential-hosted variant, where Consequential manages the Cloudflare/DNS side for you. If you'd rather manage your own DNS and hosting, see the [general Pixel (CDP) setup guide](/how-tos/cdp/how-to-setup-consequential-pixel-cdp/) instead.
</Aside>

## Before you get started

You need the following access to complete the setup:

- **Access to your DNS provider:** where you add DNS records for the site you want to add. This step certifies you as the first party for your setup.
- **Channel credentials:** for example, to add Meta you need a pixel ID and permission to access the pixel.
- **Access to your website code:** you add the snippet code and the events you want to capture to your site.

With everything ready, you can complete the entire onboarding in less than an hour.

## Set up your custom domain (DNS records)

<Steps>

1. Log in to the DNS provider that hosts your site. See the provider guides for [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/en-gb/learning/dns/dns-records/dns-cname-record/) and [GoDaddy](https://www.godaddy.com/help/add-a-cname-record-19236).

2. Add the CNAME and TXT records provided by the Consequential team to your domain provider. Consequential uses these to redirect CDP events.

3. Once you enter and verify your domain, you receive two DNS records (one TXT record and one CNAME record). Enter both into your DNS to certify your setup as first party.

   - The **TXT** record verifies ownership of the domain you entered.
   - The **CNAME** record maps your new first-party subdomain to the edge and creates a certificate for your domain.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
Depending on your DNS provider, the TXT record may require your domain as a suffix — for example, `_cf-custom-hostname.<your-subdomain>.mywebsite.com`.
</Aside>

## Install the EdgeTag app on Shopify

Install the [EdgeTag app](https://apps.shopify.com/edgetag) and log in using the email address shared by the Consequential team (default: [support@consequential.io](mailto:support@consequential.io)).

## Select your platform

In the first step, select the platform of your site. Select **Others**, because Consequential hosts the CDP.

## Set up your site/app domain

<Steps>

1. If Consequential hosts your site, select **I want to use my Cloudflare account**.

2. If you are not using custom hosting, enter your site domain. For example, for `https://www.mysite.com`, enter `mysite.com` (remove `https://` and `www.`).

3. Enter the email address provided by Consequential.

</Steps>

## Set up your DNS

With Consequential hosting, you don't need to complete this step. Select **Skip for now** for both DNS records (CNAME and TXT). The DNS entries you set earlier validate, and you see a confirmation that mapping completes correctly. Then move to the next step.

## Consent

Consent is important to Consequential, which respects user privacy. The system guards all events by consent, so it never sends data without consent.

## Events

The final step enables App Embed in your theme so Consequential can capture events in the store.

<Steps>

1. Click the **Theme customization** link to open the theme settings.

2. Locate the EdgeTag app and toggle it on.

3. Click **Save** in the top-right corner.

4. After the save succeeds, close the tab and click **Finish**.

</Steps>

You then see the dashboard, including a prompt to add your first channel.

## Next steps

Installation is now complete. In the next step of onboarding, select the channels you want to add. Refer to the documentation for each channel for detailed steps.
