This guide covers prerequisites, platform selection, domain setup, DNS configuration, and consent for the Out of the Blue Pixel (EdgeTag).

## Before you get started

Starting with OTB Pixel (EdgeTag) is like working with any other JS/iOS/Android SDK. Enter your business email and the system walks you through setup step by step. You can add your teammates once you complete setup.

You need the following access to complete the setup:

- **Access to your DNS provider:** where you add DNS records for the site you want to add. This step certifies that you are the first party to the internet for your setup.
- **Channel credentials:** for example, to add Meta you need a pixel ID and permission to access the pixel, available through setup.
- **Access to your website code:** you add the snippet code and desired events to your site.

Once you have these ready, go through onboarding. You can complete the entire process in less than an hour if you have all aspects handy.

## Select your platform

In the first step, select the platform of your site, such as Shopify, WooCommerce, or another platform.

## Set up your site/app domain

<Steps>

1. Enter your site domain. If your site is `https://www.mysite.com`, enter `mysite.com`. Remove `https://` and `www.`
2. Click **Verify Domain**. The system checks if the domain is valid and creates a first-party subdomain where you will have the OTB Pixel (EdgeTag) system.

</Steps>

For related setup, see [Cloudflare Hosting](https://support.devrev.ai/outoftheblue/settings/knowledge-base/articles/ART-1302).

## Set up your DNS

Once you enter and verify your domain, you receive two DNS records (1 TXT record and 1 CNAME record) that you must enter into your DNS to certify your setup as first party.

<Steps>

1. Add the TXT record to verify ownership of the domain you entered in the previous step.
2. Click **Next record** and add the CNAME record. This record maps your newly created first-party subdomain to the edge and creates a certificate for your domain.
3. Click **Verify Records** after adding both records. A green box confirms everything looks great.

</Steps>

## Consent

Consent is important because Out of the Blue respects users' privacy. All events sent from the system are guarded by consent, so no data is sent without consent.

## Next step

In the next step of onboarding, select the channels you want to add. Refer to the documentation of each channel for detailed steps.
